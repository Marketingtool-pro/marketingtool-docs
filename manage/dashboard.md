# Dashboard

The dashboard is the first screen after sign-in, on every platform.

* **Personalized recommendations** based on your profile and recent activity
* **Trending tools** — what's working for users like you
* **Your credit balance** — always visible, always live
* **Quick access** to recent tools and results

On the web, the dashboard also links to account, billing, and full history.
