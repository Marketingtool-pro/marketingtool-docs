# Account settings

* **Profile** — update your name and how you describe your role (this tunes your recommendations).
* **Email** — your email is your sign-in identity; contact support to change it.
* **Sessions** — sign out from the profile screen on any device.
* **Delete account** — contact [Help@marketingtool.pro](mailto:Help@marketingtool.pro) and we'll take care of it.
