# History & downloads

* Every tool run is stored in your **History**, on all platforms.
* Reopen any past result to review, copy, or continue iterating.
* On the desktop web app, use **Download All** on a result page to save every generated asset in one go.
* History is account-wide: a run made on your phone is waiting for you on the web, and vice versa.
