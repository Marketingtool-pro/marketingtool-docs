# Chat with MarketBot

**MarketBot** is the MarketingTool.Pro AI assistant, built into the app and the web dashboard.

## What MarketBot can do

* Answer marketing questions in plain language
* Recommend the right tool for the job you describe
* Help you interpret a tool's results and decide next steps

## Tips for better answers

* Be specific: "my Facebook CPA doubled this week" beats "ads not working".
* Mention your platform (Google Ads, Meta, Shopify…) so MarketBot picks the right tools to suggest.
* You can hand off any MarketBot suggestion straight into the recommended tool.
