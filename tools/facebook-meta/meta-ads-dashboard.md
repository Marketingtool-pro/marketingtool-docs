# Meta Ads Dashboard

Cross-channel reporting dashboard that unifies Meta Ads, Google Ads, GA4, Shopify, Klaviyo, and TikTok data into real-time, always-updated dashboards — replacing manual spreadsheets and fragmented reports.

**Category:** [Facebook & Meta Ads](../facebook-meta.md) · **Badge:** Facebook/Meta

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-ads-dashboard](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-ads-dashboard)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Meta Ads Dashboard”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ad Account ID or Business Name | textarea | ✅ |
| Connected Channels | select — Meta Ads Only, Meta + Google Ads, Meta + Google + Shopify, All Channels (Meta, Google, TikTok, Shopify, Klaviyo) | optional |
| Dashboard Type | select — Performance Overview, Channel Comparison, Revenue Attribution, Agency Multi-Client | optional |

## Tips for best results

* **Connect all channels** — Link Meta, Google, Shopify, and email platforms for a unified view of your marketing performance.
* **Use live dashboards** — Real-time data means you see performance as it happens, not hours or days later.
* **Automate client reports** — Schedule branded reports across unlimited accounts at the same price tier for agency scaling.

## How to run it

1. Open **Meta Ads Dashboard** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
