# Facebook Ads Manager

Centralized Facebook Ads Manager alternative with bulk editing, AI bidding, performance trend tracking, and unified campaign oversight that goes beyond native Meta tools.

**Category:** [Facebook & Meta Ads](../facebook-meta.md) · **Badge:** Facebook/Meta

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/facebook-ads-manager](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/facebook-ads-manager)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Facebook Ads Manager”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ad Account ID | text | ✅ |
| View Level | select — Campaign, Ad Set, Ad, All Levels | optional |
| Date Range | select — Today, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, Last 90 days | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Targeting**, **Creative**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Use bulk actions** — Edit budgets, bids, and statuses across multiple campaigns simultaneously to save hours of manual work.
* **Track change history** — Monitor every modification made by your team or automations and correlate changes with performance shifts.
* **Set up alerts** — Configure performance alerts for CPA spikes, budget depletion, and ROAS drops to catch issues early.

## How to run it

1. Open **Facebook Ads Manager** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
