# Meta Hidden Insights

Uncover performance blind spots and hidden audience segments in your Meta ad account that standard Ads Manager can't reveal. Discover untapped demographics, placements, and optimization opportunities.

**Category:** [Facebook & Meta Ads](../facebook-meta.md) · **Badge:** Facebook/Meta

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-hidden-insights](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-hidden-insights)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Meta Hidden Insights”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ad Account ID | text | ✅ |
| Insight Category | select — Audience Segments, Placement Performance, Creative Analysis, Budget Efficiency, Full Account Audit | optional |
| Date Range | select — Last 7 days, Last 14 days, Last 30 days, Last 90 days | optional |

## Tips for best results

* **Check unknown segments** — Find hidden demographic segments driving conversions that you didn't intentionally target.
* **Audit placements** — Discover which placements (Feed, Stories, Reels, Audience Network) deliver the best ROI.
* **Review weekly** — New insights emerge as your campaigns accumulate data — check regularly for fresh opportunities.

## How to run it

1. Open **Meta Hidden Insights** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
