# Meta Automated Reporting

Generate and distribute white-labeled ad performance reports automatically on daily, weekly, or monthly schedules. Custom metrics, branded PDFs with your logo, and team-wide distribution built in.

**Category:** [Facebook & Meta Ads](../facebook-meta.md) · **Badge:** Facebook/Meta

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-automated-reporting](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-automated-reporting)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Meta Automated Reporting”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ad Account ID or Campaign Name | textarea | ✅ |
| Report Frequency | select — Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly | optional |
| Key Metrics to Include | select — ROAS + CPA + CTR, Revenue + Spend + Profit, Impressions + Clicks + Conversions, Full Report (All Metrics) | optional |

## Tips for best results

* **Schedule smartly** — Send weekly reports on Mondays for client reviews, and monthly summaries on the 1st for billing cycles.
* **Pick right KPIs** — Choose ROAS, CPA, and CTR as primary metrics — clients care about business impact, not vanity stats.
* **White-label everything** — Add your agency logo and name to reports for professional client-facing deliverables.

## How to run it

1. Open **Meta Automated Reporting** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
