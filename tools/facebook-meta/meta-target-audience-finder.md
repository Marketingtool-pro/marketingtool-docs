# Meta Target Audience Finder

AI-powered audience discovery that explores 100+ targeting opportunities, launches preset audiences built on eRFM modeling, and uses predictive analytics to identify your next best Facebook audience.

**Category:** [Facebook & Meta Ads](../facebook-meta.md) · **Badge:** Facebook/Meta

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-target-audience-finder](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-target-audience-finder)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Meta Target Audience Finder”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business or Product Description | textarea | ✅ |
| Funnel Stage Focus | select — Prospecting (New Customers), Retargeting (Website Visitors), Retention (Existing Customers), Full Funnel | optional |
| Monthly Ad Budget | select — Under $1,000, $1,000 - $5,000, $5,000 - $20,000, $20,000 - $100,000, $100,000+ | optional |

## Tips for best results

* **Try predictive audiences** — AI uses supervised learning to analyze your performance and predict which audiences will convert next.
* **Test Super Lookalikes** — Blend multiple profitable lookalike audiences into one super-audience with percentages up to 20%.
* **Cover full funnel** — Set up acquisition, retargeting, and retention audiences together for complete funnel coverage.

## How to run it

1. Open **Meta Target Audience Finder** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
