# Facebook Marketing Suite

All-in-one Facebook marketing suite combining ad creation, audience targeting, performance analytics, and content scheduling into a unified workspace for streamlined social media marketing.

**Category:** [Facebook & Meta Ads](../facebook-meta.md) · **Badge:** Facebook/Meta

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/facebook-marketing-suite](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/facebook-marketing-suite)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Facebook Marketing Suite”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook Page URL or Business Name | textarea | ✅ |
| Primary Focus | select — Paid Advertising, Content Scheduling, Analytics & Reporting, Full Suite | optional |
| Platforms | select — Facebook Only, Facebook + Instagram, Facebook + Instagram + Messenger | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Targeting**, **Creative**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Connect all assets** — Link your Facebook Page, Instagram account, and ad account for a complete marketing command center.
* **Schedule content** — Plan organic and paid content together on one calendar for consistent brand messaging.
* **Track holistically** — View organic reach alongside paid performance to understand your true marketing impact.

## How to run it

1. Open **Facebook Marketing Suite** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
