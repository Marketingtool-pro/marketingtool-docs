# Meta Budget Optimizer

Intelligent Meta ad budget optimizer that automatically shifts spend from underperforming campaigns to top performers in real-time. Maximize ROAS while respecting your daily and lifetime budget limits.

**Category:** [Facebook & Meta Ads](../facebook-meta.md) · **Badge:** Facebook/Meta

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-budget-optimizer](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-budget-optimizer)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Meta Budget Optimizer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campaign or Account ID | textarea | ✅ |
| Total Daily Budget ($) | number | optional |
| Optimization Goal | select — Maximum ROAS, Lowest CPA, Maximum Conversions, Balanced (ROAS + Volume) | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Targeting**, **Creative**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Set min/max limits** — Define budget floors and ceilings per campaign so the optimizer can't overspend or starve any campaign.
* **Use CBO wisely** — Campaign Budget Optimization works best with 3-5 ad sets — too many and Meta can't distribute efficiently.
* **Review reallocation logs** — Check where budget was moved each day to understand patterns and refine your strategy.

## How to run it

1. Open **Meta Budget Optimizer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
