# Meta White Label

White-label Facebook ad management for agencies — rebrand campaigns, ad sets, and ads under your agency name. Generate branded PDF reports with your logo on automated schedules for professional client delivery.

**Category:** [Facebook & Meta Ads](../facebook-meta.md) · **Badge:** Facebook/Meta

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-white-label](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-white-label)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Meta White Label”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agency Name | text | ✅ |
| Client Ad Account ID | text | optional |
| Report Schedule | select — Weekly (Monday), Bi-Weekly, Monthly (1st), Custom Schedule | optional |

## Tips for best results

* **Brand everything** — Ensure campaigns, reports, and dashboards all carry your agency name and logo for consistency.
* **Automate client reports** — Schedule branded PDFs with funnel breakdowns and KPIs to send to clients automatically.
* **Add team members** — Grant teammates access to white-label reports for coordinated client communication.

## How to run it

1. Open **Meta White Label** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
