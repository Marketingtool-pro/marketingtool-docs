# Meta Campaign Analyzer

Deep-dive Facebook campaign analysis that audits your account structure, identifies wasted spend, finds scaling opportunities, and benchmarks performance against industry standards.

**Category:** [Facebook & Meta Ads](../facebook-meta.md) · **Badge:** Facebook/Meta

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-campaign-analyzer](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-campaign-analyzer)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Meta Campaign Analyzer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ad Account ID or Campaign URL | textarea | ✅ |
| Analysis Depth | select — Quick Audit (Top Issues), Standard Analysis, Deep Dive (All Metrics), Competitive Benchmark | optional |
| Your Industry | select — E-commerce, SaaS, Lead Gen, App Install, Local Business, Agency | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Targeting**, **Creative**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Audit structure first** — Check for account fragmentation — too many campaigns competing against each other waste budget.
* **Find waste** — Identify audiences, placements, and creatives consuming budget without generating conversions.
* **Benchmark against industry** — Compare your CPA, CTR, and ROAS against industry averages to understand where you stand.

## How to run it

1. Open **Meta Campaign Analyzer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
