# Meta Audience Launcher

Access 100+ ready-to-deploy Facebook audiences covering your entire sales funnel. Launch AI-powered preset audiences, Super Lookalikes, and data-driven demographics in just a few clicks.

**Category:** [Facebook & Meta Ads](../facebook-meta.md) · **Badge:** Facebook/Meta

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-audience-launcher](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-audience-launcher)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Meta Audience Launcher”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business Description | textarea | ✅ |
| Funnel Stage | select — Top of Funnel (Prospecting), Middle of Funnel (Retargeting), Bottom of Funnel (Retention), Full Funnel | optional |
| Audience Size Preference | select — Narrow (High Intent), Medium (Balanced), Broad (Maximum Reach) | optional |

## Tips for best results

* **Use AI Audiences** — 27 pre-built audiences based on eRFM model (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) deliver proven results.
* **Create Super Lookalikes** — Blend multiple high-converting lookalike audiences into one super-audience for broader reach.
* **Schedule launches** — Set audience launches during peak conversion windows for maximum impact.

## How to run it

1. Open **Meta Audience Launcher** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
