# Meta Comment Manager

Manage and moderate comments across all your Facebook and Instagram ad campaigns from one inbox. Auto-hide spam, respond to inquiries, and monitor sentiment to protect your brand reputation.

**Category:** [Facebook & Meta Ads](../facebook-meta.md) · **Badge:** Facebook/Meta

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-comment-manager](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-comment-manager)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Meta Comment Manager”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ad Account or Page ID | text | ✅ |
| Moderation Level | select — Light (Spam Only), Moderate (Spam + Negative), Strict (All Comments Reviewed), Custom Rules | optional |
| Auto-Reply | select — Disabled, Questions Only, All Positive Comments, All Comments | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Targeting**, **Creative**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Create keyword rules** — Auto-hide comments containing spam keywords, competitor names, or offensive language.
* **Prioritize purchase intent** — Flag comments asking about pricing, availability, and shipping for immediate response.
* **Track sentiment trends** — Monitor comment sentiment over time — sudden negativity may indicate product or creative issues.

## How to run it

1. Open **Meta Comment Manager** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
