# Meta Audience Studio

Targeting intelligence platform that analyzes your Facebook ad account performance to rank interests by profitability, predict top targeting options, and build precision audiences with drag-and-drop tools.

**Category:** [Facebook & Meta Ads](../facebook-meta.md) · **Badge:** Facebook/Meta

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-audience-studio](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-audience-studio)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Meta Audience Studio”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ad Account ID or Niche | textarea | ✅ |
| Analysis Type | select — Interest Discovery, Audience Overlap Check, Profitability Ranking, Full Account Audit | optional |
| Data Lookback Period | select — Last 30 days, Last 60 days, Last 90 days, Last 180 days | optional |

## Tips for best results

* **Discover hidden interests** — AI analyzes your account to rank interests by profitability and predict your next best targeting option.
* **Use audience builder** — Drag-and-drop interface lets you intersect and exclude audiences for laser-focused targeting.
* **Launch directly** — Deploy audiences immediately or save them as presets for rapid campaign launches.

## How to run it

1. Open **Meta Audience Studio** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
