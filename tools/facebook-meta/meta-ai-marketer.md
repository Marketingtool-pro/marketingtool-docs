# Meta AI Marketer

Your personal AI advertising consultant that audits Meta ad accounts 24/7, delivers data-driven optimization recommendations, and prepares campaigns for one-click launch — eliminating costly manual mistakes.

**Category:** [Facebook & Meta Ads](../facebook-meta.md) · **Badge:** Facebook/Meta

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-ai-marketer](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-ai-marketer)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Meta AI Marketer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ad Account ID or Business URL | textarea | ✅ |
| Primary Goal | select — Increase ROAS, Lower CPA, Scale Spend, Improve CTR, Reduce Waste | optional |
| Monthly Ad Budget ($) | number | optional |

## Tips for best results

* **Let AI audit daily** — The AI continuously monitors your account to catch optimization opportunities human eyes miss.
* **One-click launch** — AI prepares ads, audiences, and campaign elements — you just review and click launch.
* **Scale safely** — AI identifies growth opportunities while protecting your budget from inefficient spending.

## How to run it

1. Open **Meta AI Marketer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
