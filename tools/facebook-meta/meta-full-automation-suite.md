# Meta Full Automation Suite

Comprehensive Facebook ad automation and optimization software that combines AI bidding, audience management, creative rotation, and budget allocation into one unified platform for hands-free campaign management.

**Category:** [Facebook & Meta Ads](../facebook-meta.md) · **Badge:** Facebook/Meta

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-full-automation-suite](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-full-automation-suite)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Meta Full Automation Suite”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ad Account ID | text | ✅ |
| Automation Level | select — Conservative (Alerts Only), Moderate (Suggest + Apply), Aggressive (Full Autopilot) | optional |
| Primary KPI | select — ROAS, CPA, Revenue, Conversions, Profit | optional |

## Tips for best results

* **Enable all modules** — Activate bidding, audience, creative, and budget automation together for maximum hands-free optimization.
* **Set clear KPIs** — Define your target ROAS, CPA, or CPL upfront so the automation has clear goals to optimize toward.
* **Monitor weekly** — Review automation performance reports weekly and adjust parameters based on business changes.

## How to run it

1. Open **Meta Full Automation Suite** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
