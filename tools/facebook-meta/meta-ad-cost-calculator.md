# Meta Ad Cost Calculator

Calculate estimated Facebook and Instagram ad costs based on your target audience, placement, objective, and industry benchmarks. Plan budgets with confidence before launching campaigns.

**Category:** [Facebook & Meta Ads](../facebook-meta.md) · **Badge:** Facebook/Meta

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-ad-cost-calculator](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-ad-cost-calculator)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Meta Ad Cost Calculator”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campaign Objective | select — Conversions, Traffic, Lead Generation, Brand Awareness, App Installs, Video Views | ✅ |
| Daily Budget ($) | number | optional |
| Industry | select — E-commerce, SaaS, Finance, Healthcare, Education, Real Estate | optional |
| Target Location | text | optional |

## Tips for best results

* **Set realistic budgets** — Use industry benchmarks as starting points, then adjust based on your historical performance data.
* **Factor in seasonality** — Ad costs spike during Q4, Black Friday, and major shopping events — plan accordingly.
* **Test small first** — Start with a smaller daily budget to gather data before scaling up your spend.

## How to run it

1. Open **Meta Ad Cost Calculator** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
