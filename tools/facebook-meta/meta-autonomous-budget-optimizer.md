# Meta Autonomous Budget Optimizer

AI-powered budget optimization that automatically redistributes Facebook ad spend to top-performing campaigns, ad sets, and creatives in real-time — maximizing ROAS without manual intervention.

**Category:** [Facebook & Meta Ads](../facebook-meta.md) · **Badge:** Facebook/Meta

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-autonomous-budget-optimizer](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-autonomous-budget-optimizer)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Meta Autonomous Budget Optimizer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campaign or Account to Optimize | textarea | ✅ |
| Total Daily Budget ($) | number | optional |
| Optimize For | select — Maximum ROAS, Lowest CPA, Maximum Conversions, Maximum Revenue | optional |

## Tips for best results

* **Set guardrails** — Define minimum and maximum budget limits per ad set so AI optimizes within your comfort zone.
* **Trust the data** — AI needs 3-7 days of data before making confident budget shifts — don't override too early.
* **Review weekly** — Check budget allocation reports weekly to understand AI decision patterns and adjust limits if needed.

## How to run it

1. Open **Meta Autonomous Budget Optimizer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
