# Meta Ad Launcher

Launch hundreds of winning Facebook and Instagram ads instantly by combining top-performing creatives with high-converting ad copy. Convert organic posts into paid ads with one click.

**Category:** [Facebook & Meta Ads](../facebook-meta.md) · **Badge:** Facebook/Meta

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-ad-launcher](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/meta-ad-launcher)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Meta Ad Launcher”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ad Creative Description | textarea | ✅ |
| Ad Format | select — Single Image, Carousel, Collection | optional |
| Campaign Objective | select — Conversions, Traffic, Engagement, Lead Generation, Sales | optional |
| Number of Variations | select — 5, 10, 20, 50 | optional |

## Tips for best results

* **Combine winners** — Merge your best visuals with top-performing copy to create new high-potential ad variations.
* **Use post-to-ad** — Convert organic posts that already have social proof into paid advertisements for better performance.
* **Pre-set ad clusters** — Save templated ad combinations for different audiences to reduce future setup time.

## How to run it

1. Open **Meta Ad Launcher** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
