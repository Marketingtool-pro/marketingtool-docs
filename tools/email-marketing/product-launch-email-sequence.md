# Product Launch Email Sequence

Create launch email sequences.

**Category:** [Email Marketing](../email-marketing.md) · **Badge:** Email

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/product-launch-email-sequence](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/product-launch-email-sequence)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Product Launch Email Sequence”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Email Subject | text | optional |
| Call to Action | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Compose**, **Personalize**, **Sequence**.

## Tips for best results

* **Know Your Recipient** — Describe who you are writing to for personalization.
* **State Your Goal** — Define the email purpose: sell, inform, or follow up.
* **Keep It Short** — Best emails are 50-125 words for optimal engagement.

## How to run it

1. Open **Product Launch Email Sequence** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
