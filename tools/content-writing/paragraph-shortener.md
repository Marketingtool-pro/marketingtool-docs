# Paragraph Shortener

AI-powered paragraph shortener.

**Category:** [Content & Copywriting](../content-writing.md) · **Badge:** Text Editing

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/paragraph-shortener](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/paragraph-shortener)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Paragraph Shortener”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Transform**, **Style**, **Export**.

## Tips for best results

* **Paste Original Text** — Include the exact text you want transformed.
* **Set Target Tone** — Choose formal, casual, or technical style.
* **Specify Audience** — Mention who will read it for appropriate level.

## How to run it

1. Open **Paragraph Shortener** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
