# Youtube Ad Script Writer

Write compelling YouTube ad scripts.

**Category:** [Social Media & Channels](../social-media.md) · **Badge:** YouTube

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/youtube-ad-script-writer](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/youtube-ad-script-writer)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Youtube Ad Script Writer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Video Topic | text | optional |
| Video Duration | select — Short (<1 min), Medium (1-5 min), Long (5-15 min), Extended (15+ min) | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Writing**, **Editing**, **Publish**.

## Tips for best results

* **Define Your Topic** — Be specific about the subject for focused content.
* **Set Target Length** — Specify word count for appropriate depth.
* **Know Your Reader** — Describe your audience for the right tone and level.

## How to run it

1. Open **Youtube Ad Script Writer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
