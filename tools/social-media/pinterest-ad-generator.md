# Pinterest Ad Generator

Create stunning Pinterest ad content.

**Category:** [Social Media & Channels](../social-media.md) · **Badge:** Pinterest

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/pinterest-ad-generator](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/pinterest-ad-generator)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Pinterest Ad Generator”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Writing**, **Editing**, **Publish**.

## Tips for best results

* **Name Your Platform** — Specify which social platform for optimized output.
* **Describe Your Brand** — Share your brand voice for consistent messaging.
* **Include Context** — Mention the occasion, trend, or campaign theme.

## How to run it

1. Open **Pinterest Ad Generator** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
