# Marketing Calendar

AI-powered social media marketing calendar that plans content themes, posting schedules, campaign launches, and promotional events across all your social channels with drag-and-drop simplicity.

**Category:** [Social Media & Channels](../social-media.md) · **Badge:** Social Media

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/marketing-calendar](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/marketing-calendar)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Marketing Calendar”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business & Content Strategy | textarea | ✅ |
| Planning Period | select — 1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month, 3 months | optional |
| Channels to Include | select — Instagram Only, Instagram + Facebook, All Social Channels, Instagram + TikTok + YouTube | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Competitors**, **Trends**, **Strategy**.

## Tips for best results

* **Plan around events** — Map industry events, holidays, and trending moments into your calendar for timely, relevant content.
* **Batch content creation** — Dedicate 1-2 days per month to creating all content, then schedule it across the calendar for consistency.
* **Leave room for spontaneity** — Schedule 80% of content in advance, but leave 20% open for trending topics and real-time engagement.

## How to run it

1. Open **Marketing Calendar** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
