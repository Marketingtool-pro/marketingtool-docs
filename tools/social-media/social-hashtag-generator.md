# Social Hashtag Generator

Advanced hashtag research and generation tool that analyzes trending hashtags, competition levels, and audience overlap to create optimized hashtag strategies for maximum social media discoverability.

**Category:** [Social Media & Channels](../social-media.md) · **Badge:** Social Media

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/social-hashtag-generator](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/social-hashtag-generator)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Social Hashtag Generator”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Content Topic or Niche | textarea | ✅ |
| Hashtag Strategy | select — Maximum Reach, Niche Targeting, Trending Now, Competitor Analysis, Balanced Mix | optional |
| Platform | select — Instagram, TikTok, Twitter/X, YouTube, All Platforms | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Writing**, **Editing**, **Publish**.

## Tips for best results

* **Research competitors** — Analyze which hashtags your top competitors use and which ones drive their highest engagement posts.
* **Track hashtag performance** — Monitor which of your hashtags actually drive impressions from Explore and hashtag searches.
* **Create hashtag groups** — Build themed hashtag groups (product, lifestyle, industry, location) to rotate across posts.

## How to run it

1. Open **Social Hashtag Generator** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
