# Post Scheduler

Schedule social media posts across Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, and Twitter with AI-powered optimal timing. Auto-publish at the exact moments your audience is most active for maximum reach.

**Category:** [Social Media & Channels](../social-media.md) · **Badge:** Social Media

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/post-scheduler](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/post-scheduler)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Post Scheduler”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Post Content | textarea | ✅ |
| Platforms | select — Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, All Platforms | optional |
| Posting Time | select — AI Optimal Time, Morning (8-10am), Lunch (11am-1pm), Afternoon (3-5pm), Evening (7-9pm), Custom Time | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Create**, **Schedule**, **Analytics**.

## Tips for best results

* **Use AI timing** — Let AI analyze your historical data to find the exact posting times when your followers are most active.
* **Queue a week ahead** — Always maintain at least 7 days of scheduled content to ensure consistency even when you're busy.
* **Preview before scheduling** — Check how posts look on mobile before scheduling — formatting issues are common across platforms.

## How to run it

1. Open **Post Scheduler** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
