# Social Media Post Generator

Generate complete social media posts with AI — captions, hashtags, emoji placement, and call-to-actions optimized for each platform. Creates Instagram carousels, TikTok scripts, LinkedIn articles, and Twitter threads.

**Category:** [Social Media & Channels](../social-media.md) · **Badge:** Social Media

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/social-media-post-generator](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/social-media-post-generator)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Social Media Post Generator”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Post Topic or Content Brief | textarea | ✅ |
| Platform | select — Instagram (Feed), Instagram (Reel Script), Instagram (Carousel), TikTok Script, LinkedIn Post, Twitter Thread | optional |
| Number of Posts | select — 1 post, 3 posts, 5 posts, 10 posts | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Writing**, **Editing**, **Publish**.

## Tips for best results

* **Specify your platform** — Each platform has different formats, character limits, and audience expectations — generate per platform for best results.
* **Include visual direction** — AI also suggests image/video concepts to pair with the text for higher-performing posts.
* **Generate in batches** — Create 10-15 posts at once around a content theme for a week's worth of content in minutes.

## How to run it

1. Open **Social Media Post Generator** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
