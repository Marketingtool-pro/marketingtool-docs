# Social Analytics Dashboard

Unified social media analytics dashboard tracking followers, engagement, reach, impressions, and revenue across Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and YouTube with beautiful visual reports and benchmarking.

**Category:** [Social Media & Channels](../social-media.md) · **Badge:** Social Media

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/social-analytics-dashboard](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/social-analytics-dashboard)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Social Analytics Dashboard”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Social Media Handles | textarea | ✅ |
| Platforms to Track | select — Instagram Only, Instagram + Facebook, All Meta Platforms, All Social Platforms | optional |
| Report Type | select — Weekly Summary, Monthly Deep Dive, Quarterly Review, Custom Date Range | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Create**, **Schedule**, **Analytics**.

## Tips for best results

* **Focus on growth metrics** — Track follower growth rate (%), engagement rate, and reach per post — not just total follower count.
* **Compare platforms** — Side-by-side platform comparison shows where your content performs best and where to invest more.
* **Export for clients** — Generate branded PDF reports for clients or stakeholders with one click — no manual spreadsheet work.

## How to run it

1. Open **Social Analytics Dashboard** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
