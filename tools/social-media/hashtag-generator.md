# Hashtag Generator

Generate research-backed hashtag sets for Instagram and TikTok that maximize discoverability. AI analyzes hashtag volume, competition, and relevance to find the perfect mix of broad, niche, and branded tags.

**Category:** [Social Media & Channels](../social-media.md) · **Badge:** Social Media

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/hashtag-generator](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/hashtag-generator)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Hashtag Generator”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Post Topic or Niche | textarea | ✅ |
| Platform | select — Instagram, TikTok, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Universal | optional |
| Hashtag Count | select — 10 hashtags, 15 hashtags, 20 hashtags, 30 hashtags (Instagram max) | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Writing**, **Editing**, **Publish**.

## Tips for best results

* **Mix hashtag sizes** — Use 10 large (1M+ posts), 10 medium (100K-1M), and 10 small (10K-100K) hashtags for optimal reach.
* **Rotate sets** — Don't use the same 30 hashtags every post — create 5 different sets and rotate to avoid shadowban risk.
* **Include branded tags** — Create a unique branded hashtag for your business and include it in every post for community building.

## How to run it

1. Open **Hashtag Generator** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
