# Ai Paid Social Manager

AI-powered paid social media campaign manager that automates ad creation, audience targeting, budget allocation, and performance optimization across Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and LinkedIn simultaneously.

**Category:** [Social Media & Channels](../social-media.md) · **Badge:** Social Media

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ai-paid-social-manager](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ai-paid-social-manager)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Ai Paid Social Manager”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campaign Brief | textarea | ✅ |
| Platforms | select — Instagram Only, Instagram + Facebook, All Meta Platforms, Instagram + TikTok, All Platforms | optional |
| Monthly Budget ($) | number | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Create**, **Schedule**, **Analytics**.

## Tips for best results

* **Unify cross-platform** — Manage campaigns across all social platforms from one dashboard to maintain consistent messaging and budgets.
* **Let AI allocate budget** — AI distributes spend to the best-performing platform and audience combinations in real-time.
* **Test platform-native formats** — Each platform has unique ad formats — Reels on Instagram, Stories on Facebook, Spark Ads on TikTok.

## How to run it

1. Open **Ai Paid Social Manager** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
