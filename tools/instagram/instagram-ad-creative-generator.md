## Tips for best results

* **Design for mobile** — 97% of Instagram users are on mobile — always design and preview on a phone screen first.
* **Use UGC style** — User-generated content style ads outperform polished brand ads by 4x on Instagram — keep it authentic.
* **Test video vs static** — Video ads get 38% more engagement than static images on Instagram — test both and scale the winner.
