# Instagram Caption Generator

Create scroll-stopping Instagram captions with AI that understands your brand voice, audience psychology, and platform best practices. Generate captions for Feed posts, Reels, Stories, and carousels with optimized hashtags.

**Category:** [Instagram](../instagram.md) · **Badge:** Instagram

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/instagram-caption-generator](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/instagram-caption-generator)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Instagram Caption Generator”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Post Topic or Image Description | textarea | ✅ |
| Caption Tone | select — Casual & Fun, Professional, Inspirational, Educational, Witty & Humorous, Storytelling | optional |
| Post Type | select — Feed Post, Reel, Carousel, Story, IGTV | optional |
| Hashtag Count | select — 5 hashtags, 10 hashtags, 15 hashtags, 20 hashtags, 30 hashtags (max) | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Targeting**, **Creative**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Hook in first line** — The first line must stop the scroll — use a question, bold statement, or number to grab attention before the 'more' cutoff.
* **Include a CTA** — Every caption should end with a clear call-to-action: save, share, comment, click link in bio, or DM.
* **Use line breaks** — Break text into short paragraphs with spacing for easy mobile readability.

## How to run it

1. Open **Instagram Caption Generator** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
