# Instagram Growth Strategy

Get a personalized Instagram growth strategy based on your niche, current metrics, and goals. AI analyzes your account and generates a 30/60/90 day action plan for followers, engagement, and monetization.

**Category:** [Instagram](../instagram.md) · **Badge:** Instagram

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/instagram-growth-strategy](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/instagram-growth-strategy)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Instagram Growth Strategy”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account Description & Current Stats | textarea | ✅ |
| Current Followers | select — 0-1K, 1K-5K, 5K-10K, 10K-50K, 50K-100K, 100K+ | optional |
| Primary Goal | select — Rapid Follower Growth, Increase Engagement Rate, Monetize Account, Build Email List, Drive Website Traffic | optional |

## Tips for best results

* **Consistency beats virality** — Posting 5-7 times per week consistently grows accounts faster than occasional viral hits.
* **Engage before posting** — Spend 15 minutes engaging with others' content before you post — it primes the algorithm.
* **Collaborate weekly** — One collaboration per week (Collab posts, Lives, Reel duets) exposes you to new audiences.

## How to run it

1. Open **Instagram Growth Strategy** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
