## Tips for best results

* **Use 20-25 hashtags** — Research shows 20-25 hashtags perform better than using the full 30 — quality over quantity.
* **Check banned hashtags** — Some innocent-looking hashtags are banned or restricted — always verify before using.
* **Track what works** — Use Insights to check which hashtags drive the most impressions from hashtag search.
