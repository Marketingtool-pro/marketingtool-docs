# Instagram Ai Automation

Automate your Instagram marketing workflow — schedule posts, auto-engage with followers, manage DMs, and trigger actions based on comments and story interactions to grow your account on autopilot.

**Category:** [Instagram](../instagram.md) · **Badge:** Instagram

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/instagram-ai-automation](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/instagram-ai-automation)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Instagram Ai Automation”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram Handle or Business Description | textarea | ✅ |
| Automation Type | select — Post Scheduling, Comment Auto-Reply, DM Automation, Story Interactions, Full Workflow | optional |
| Posting Frequency | select — Daily, 3x per week, 5x per week, Multiple per day | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Targeting**, **Creative**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Auto-engage safely** — Set natural interaction patterns with delays between actions to avoid Instagram's automation detection limits.
* **Schedule for peak hours** — Post when your audience is most active — typically 11am-1pm and 7pm-9pm in your target timezone.
* **Automate DM responses** — Set up keyword-triggered DM replies for common inquiries about pricing, availability, and links.

## How to run it

1. Open **Instagram Ai Automation** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
