# Instagram Story Ads Manager

Create and manage high-converting Instagram Story ads with full-screen immersive formats. Design swipe-up sequences, interactive polls, quiz stickers, and countdown timers that drive action and engagement.

**Category:** [Instagram](../instagram.md) · **Badge:** Instagram

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/instagram-story-ads-manager](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/instagram-story-ads-manager)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Instagram Story Ads Manager”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ad Campaign Description | textarea | ✅ |
| Story Format | select — Single Image, Single Video, Carousel (Multi-Frame), Interactive (Poll/Quiz), Collection | optional |
| Campaign Objective | select — Traffic (Swipe Up), Conversions, App Installs, Lead Generation, Brand Awareness | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Targeting**, **Creative**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Design for vertical** — Stories are 9:16 — design specifically for this format, never repurpose square or horizontal content.
* **Add interactive elements** — Polls, quizzes, and emoji sliders increase engagement 3x and signal to the algorithm that your ad is valuable.
* **Keep text minimal** — Stories are consumed fast — use large, bold text with a clear CTA. Aim for under 15 words per frame.

## How to run it

1. Open **Instagram Story Ads Manager** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
