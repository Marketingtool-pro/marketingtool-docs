# Instagram Engagement Analyzer

Deep-dive analytics for your Instagram engagement — track likes, comments, saves, shares, story interactions, and Reel plays. Identify what content types, posting times, and hashtags drive the most meaningful engagement.

**Category:** [Instagram](../instagram.md) · **Badge:** Instagram

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/instagram-engagement-analyzer](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/instagram-engagement-analyzer)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Instagram Engagement Analyzer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram Handle | text | ✅ |
| Analysis Focus | select — Overall Engagement, Reels Performance, Stories Analytics, Hashtag Effectiveness, Audience Growth | optional |
| Date Range | select — Last 7 days, Last 14 days, Last 30 days, Last 90 days | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Targeting**, **Creative**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Track saves over likes** — Saves indicate high-value content that users want to revisit — Instagram's algorithm weights saves heavily.
* **Monitor story drop-off** — Check where viewers exit your story sequence to optimize the number of frames and content flow.
* **Benchmark weekly** — Compare engagement rates week-over-week to spot trends before they become problems.

## How to run it

1. Open **Instagram Engagement Analyzer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
