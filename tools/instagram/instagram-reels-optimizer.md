## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Targeting**, **Creative**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Hook in 0.5 seconds** — The first half-second determines if viewers watch or scroll — start with motion, text, or a surprising visual.
* **Use trending audio** — Reels with trending audio get 2-5x more distribution — check the Reels tab for audio with the trending arrow.
* **Keep it 7-15 seconds** — Short Reels get more replays (which counts as views), boosting the algorithm signal for wider distribution.
