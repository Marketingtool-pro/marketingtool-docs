# Instagram Competitor Analyzer

Analyze your Instagram competitors' strategies — their posting frequency, content types, engagement patterns, top-performing posts, hashtag usage, and growth rate to find opportunities they're missing.

**Category:** [Instagram](../instagram.md) · **Badge:** Instagram

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/instagram-competitor-analyzer](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/instagram-competitor-analyzer)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Instagram Competitor Analyzer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Competitor Handles | textarea | ✅ |
| Analysis Depth | select — Quick Overview, Standard Analysis, Deep Dive (All Metrics) | optional |
| Timeframe | select — Last 30 days, Last 60 days, Last 90 days, Last 6 months | optional |

## Tips for best results

* **Track 3-5 competitors** — Monitor direct competitors plus 1-2 aspirational accounts to benchmark against and find content gaps.
* **Study their top posts** — Sort competitor posts by engagement to understand what content resonates with your shared audience.
* **Find posting gaps** — Identify topics and content formats your competitors aren't covering — these are your opportunities.

## How to run it

1. Open **Instagram Competitor Analyzer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
