# Instagram Content Scheduler

Plan and schedule your entire Instagram content calendar weeks in advance. Visual grid preview shows how your feed will look, with optimal posting times calculated from your audience analytics.

**Category:** [Instagram](../instagram.md) · **Badge:** Instagram

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/instagram-content-scheduler](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/instagram-content-scheduler)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Instagram Content Scheduler”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Content Plan Description | textarea | ✅ |
| Posts Per Week | select — 3 posts, 5 posts, 7 posts (daily), 10+ posts, 14 posts (2x daily) | optional |
| Content Mix | select — Balanced (Reels + Carousels + Images), Reels-Heavy (60% Reels), Carousel-Heavy (60% Carousels), Custom Mix | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Targeting**, **Creative**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Plan 2 weeks ahead** — Batch-create content biweekly so you always have a buffer and never scramble for last-minute posts.
* **Mix content types** — Alternate between carousels (highest saves), Reels (highest reach), and single images (brand consistency).
* **Preview your grid** — Check the visual feed preview before scheduling to ensure aesthetic consistency across your profile.

## How to run it

1. Open **Instagram Content Scheduler** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
