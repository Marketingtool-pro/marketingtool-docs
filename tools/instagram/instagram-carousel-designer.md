# Instagram Carousel Designer

Design high-performing Instagram carousel posts that drive saves and shares. AI generates slide-by-slide content outlines with hook, value, and CTA structure optimized for the carousel format's unique engagement pattern.

**Category:** [Instagram](../instagram.md) · **Badge:** Instagram

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/instagram-carousel-designer](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/instagram-carousel-designer)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Instagram Carousel Designer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Carousel Topic | textarea | ✅ |
| Number of Slides | select — 5 slides, 7 slides, 10 slides | optional |
| Design Style | select — Clean & Minimal, Bold & Colorful, Professional, Playful, Dark & Premium | optional |

## Tips for best results

* **Strong first slide** — The first slide is your headline — make it bold, curiosity-driven, and impossible to scroll past.
* **End with a CTA** — Last slide should always have a clear CTA: 'Save for later', 'Share with a friend', 'Follow for more'.
* **Use 7-10 slides** — 7-10 slides perform best — enough to deliver value but not so many that viewers lose interest.

1. Open **Instagram Carousel Designer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
