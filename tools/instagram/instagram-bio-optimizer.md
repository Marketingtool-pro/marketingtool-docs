# Instagram Bio Optimizer

Optimize your Instagram bio for maximum profile visits, follows, and link clicks. AI analyzes top-performing bios in your niche to craft a compelling 150-character bio with strategic keywords, emojis, and CTA.

**Category:** [Instagram](../instagram.md) · **Badge:** Instagram

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/instagram-bio-optimizer](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/instagram-bio-optimizer)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Instagram Bio Optimizer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business Description | textarea | ✅ |
| Bio Style | select — Professional, Creative & Fun, Minimalist, Bold & Direct, Storytelling | optional |
| Include Emojis? | select — Yes - Relevant emojis, Minimal - 1-2 emojis, No emojis | optional |

## Tips for best results

* **Front-load keywords** — Put your main keyword (e.g., 'Fitness Coach') at the start of your name field — it's searchable and ranks in Instagram search.
* **Clear CTA** — End with a specific action: 'DM for pricing', 'Shop below', 'Free guide in link' — tell visitors exactly what to do.
* **Use line breaks** — Structure your bio with line breaks for easy scanning — Name | What you do | Who you help | CTA.

## How to run it

1. Open **Instagram Bio Optimizer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
