# Instagram Influencer Finder

Discover and vet Instagram influencers for brand partnerships. Search by niche, location, audience demographics, engagement rate, and follower authenticity to find creators who deliver real ROI.

**Category:** [Instagram](../instagram.md) · **Badge:** Instagram

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/instagram-influencer-finder](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/instagram-influencer-finder)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Instagram Influencer Finder”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Niche or Product Category | textarea | ✅ |
| Follower Range | select — Nano (1K-10K), Micro (10K-50K), Mid-Tier (50K-500K), Macro (500K-1M), Mega (1M+) | optional |
| Target Location | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Targeting**, **Creative**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Check engagement rate** — Look for 3-6% engagement rate for micro-influencers (10K-100K) — anything below 1% suggests fake followers.
* **Verify audience quality** — Check follower demographics match your target market — a fitness influencer's audience should care about fitness.
* **Start with micro-influencers** — Micro-influencers (10K-50K) often deliver higher ROI than mega-influencers due to trust and niche relevance.

## How to run it

1. Open **Instagram Influencer Finder** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
