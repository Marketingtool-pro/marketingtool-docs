# Ad Budget Allocator

Uses AI to dynamically allocate ad budget across Meta, Google, TikTok, and Pinterest based on real-time ROAS performance data.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** E-commerce

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ad-budget-allocator](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ad-budget-allocator)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Ad Budget Allocator”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Store / Brand Name | text | optional |
| Monthly Budget | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Allocation**, **Performance**, **Simulate**.

## Tips for best results

* **Total Budget** — What is your total monthly ad budget?
* **Current Split** — How is budget currently split across channels?
* **Goal** — Maximize ROAS, minimize CAC, or scale volume?

## How to run it

1. Open **Ad Budget Allocator** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
