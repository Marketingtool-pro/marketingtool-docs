# Shopify Product Page Enhancer

Rewrites product titles, descriptions, and bullet points using AI to maximize SEO visibility and conversion rate.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** Shopify

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/shopify-product-page-enhancer](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/shopify-product-page-enhancer)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Shopify Product Page Enhancer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Product Name | text | optional |
| Target Keywords | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Titles**, **Descriptions**, **Bullets**.

## Tips for best results

* **Product URL** — Share the product page you want to enhance.
* **Target Keywords** — What search terms should this product rank for?
* **Unique Selling Points** — What makes your product stand out from competitors?

## How to run it

1. Open **Shopify Product Page Enhancer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
