# Marketing Efficiency Ratio Tracker

Tracks your MER (total revenue / total ad spend) with AI-driven insights on how to improve overall marketing efficiency.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** E-commerce

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/marketing-efficiency-ratio-tracker](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/marketing-efficiency-ratio-tracker)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Marketing Efficiency Ratio Tracker”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Store / Brand Name | text | optional |
| Monthly Revenue | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **MER**, **Benchmarks**, **Insights**.

## Tips for best results

* **Total Revenue** — Share your monthly total revenue figure.
* **Total Ad Spend** — Include all marketing costs, not just ads.
* **Industry** — Specify your niche for relevant benchmarks.

## How to run it

1. Open **Marketing Efficiency Ratio Tracker** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
