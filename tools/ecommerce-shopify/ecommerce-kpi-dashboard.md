# Ecommerce KPI Dashboard

AI-powered dashboard tracking your top 20 ecommerce KPIs — AOV, CLV, ROAS, CPC, bounce rate — with automated alerts and trend analysis.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** E-commerce

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ecommerce-kpi-dashboard](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ecommerce-kpi-dashboard)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Ecommerce KPI Dashboard”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Store / Brand Name | text | optional |
| Industry Vertical | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **KPIs**, **Alerts**, **Trends**.

## Tips for best results

* **Connect Your Store** — Link your Shopify, WooCommerce, or BigCommerce store for real-time data.
* **Set Target KPIs** — Define benchmarks so alerts fire when metrics drift.
* **Choose Time Range** — Compare week-over-week or month-over-month for trends.

## How to run it

1. Open **Ecommerce KPI Dashboard** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
