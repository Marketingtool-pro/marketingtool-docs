# Loyalty VIP Rewards Designer

Designs tiered loyalty and VIP reward programs with automated trigger emails for milestone achievements and exclusive offers.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** E-commerce

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/loyalty-vip-rewards-designer](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/loyalty-vip-rewards-designer)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Loyalty VIP Rewards Designer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Store / Brand Name | text | optional |
| Industry | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Tiers**, **Rewards**, **Emails**.

## Tips for best results

* **Number of Tiers** — How many loyalty tiers — Bronze/Silver/Gold or custom?
* **Point System** — Points per dollar spent, or purchases-based?
* **VIP Perks** — What exclusive benefits do VIPs get?

## How to run it

1. Open **Loyalty VIP Rewards Designer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
