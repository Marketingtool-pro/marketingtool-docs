# Retargeting Funnel Builder

Creates multi-stage retargeting campaigns across platforms, segmenting audiences by funnel stage — viewed, added-to-cart, abandoned checkout.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** E-commerce

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/retargeting-funnel-builder](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/retargeting-funnel-builder)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Retargeting Funnel Builder”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Store / Brand Name | text | optional |
| Ad Platforms | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Stages**, **Creatives**, **Budget**.

## Tips for best results

* **Pixel Setup** — Are Meta Pixel and Google Tag properly installed?
* **Funnel Stages** — How many retargeting stages — 2, 3, or 4?
* **Exclusion Windows** — How long after purchase do you exclude buyers?

## How to run it

1. Open **Retargeting Funnel Builder** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
