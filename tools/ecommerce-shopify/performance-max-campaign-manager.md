# Performance Max Campaign Manager

Manages and optimizes Google Performance Max campaigns with AI-driven asset group creation and audience signal tuning.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** E-commerce

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/performance-max-campaign-manager](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/performance-max-campaign-manager)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Performance Max Campaign Manager”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Store / Brand Name | text | optional |
| Campaign Goal | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Assets**, **Signals**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Asset Groups** — How many asset groups do you want to create?
* **Audience Signals** — Describe your ideal customer segments.
* **Campaign Goal** — Sales, leads, or store visits?

## How to run it

1. Open **Performance Max Campaign Manager** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
