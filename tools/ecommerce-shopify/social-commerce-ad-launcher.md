# Social Commerce Ad Launcher

Launches shoppable ad campaigns across Facebook Shop, Instagram Shop, and Pinterest with integrated product catalog sync.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** E-commerce

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/social-commerce-ad-launcher](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/social-commerce-ad-launcher)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Social Commerce Ad Launcher”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Store / Brand Name | text | optional |
| Platforms | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Catalog**, **Platforms**, **Launch**.

## Tips for best results

* **Product Catalog** — Is your catalog synced with Facebook and Pinterest?
* **Top Products** — Which products to feature in shoppable ads?
* **Budget Split** — How to allocate across FB Shop, IG Shop, Pinterest?

## How to run it

1. Open **Social Commerce Ad Launcher** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
