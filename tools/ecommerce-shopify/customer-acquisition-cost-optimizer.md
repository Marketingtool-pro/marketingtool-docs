# Customer Acquisition Cost Optimizer

Tracks CAC by channel and campaign, then uses AI to recommend the lowest-cost acquisition paths for your store.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** E-commerce

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/customer-acquisition-cost-optimizer](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/customer-acquisition-cost-optimizer)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Customer Acquisition Cost Optimizer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Store / Brand Name | text | optional |
| Current CAC | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Channels**, **Optimize**, **Forecast**.

## Tips for best results

* **Ad Spend Per Channel** — Break down your spend across Meta, Google, TikTok, etc.
* **New Customers** — How many new customers per month from each channel?
* **Target CAC** — What is your ideal cost to acquire a customer?

## How to run it

1. Open **Customer Acquisition Cost Optimizer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
