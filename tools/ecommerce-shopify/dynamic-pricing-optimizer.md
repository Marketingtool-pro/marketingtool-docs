# Dynamic Pricing Optimizer

Uses AI to recommend optimal pricing strategies based on competitor pricing, demand signals, margins, and inventory levels.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** E-commerce

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/dynamic-pricing-optimizer](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/dynamic-pricing-optimizer)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Dynamic Pricing Optimizer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Product Category | text | optional |
| Top Competitors | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Competitors**, **Strategy**, **Simulate**.

## Tips for best results

* **Product Category** — What products need pricing optimization?
* **Competitors** — List your top 3-5 competitors.
* **Margin Floor** — What is your minimum acceptable margin?

## How to run it

1. Open **Dynamic Pricing Optimizer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
