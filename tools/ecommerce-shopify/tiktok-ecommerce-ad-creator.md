# TikTok Ecommerce Ad Creator

Generates TikTok-native ad scripts, hooks, and creative briefs optimized for Gen Z shoppers and ecommerce conversion.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** E-commerce

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/tiktok-ecommerce-ad-creator](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/tiktok-ecommerce-ad-creator)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “TikTok Ecommerce Ad Creator”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Product Name | text | optional |
| Target Audience | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Script**, **Hooks**, **Brief**.

## Tips for best results

* **Product Type** — What product are you promoting?
* **Target Audience** — Age range, interests, and buying behavior.
* **Content Style** — UGC, talking head, product demo, or trend-based?

## How to run it

1. Open **TikTok Ecommerce Ad Creator** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
