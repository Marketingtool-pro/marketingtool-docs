# Cross-Sell Upsell Flow Builder

Builds automated cross-sell and upsell email sequences triggered by specific purchases, using AI to select the best product pairings.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** E-commerce

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/cross-sell-upsell-flow-builder](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/cross-sell-upsell-flow-builder)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Cross-Sell Upsell Flow Builder”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Top Products | text | optional |
| Upsell Strategy | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Pairings**, **Emails**, **Timing**.

## Tips for best results

* **Top Products** — List your best-selling products to build pairings around.
* **Complementary Items** — What accessories or add-ons go well together?
* **Price Range** — Upsell to same tier or premium products?

## How to run it

1. Open **Cross-Sell Upsell Flow Builder** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
