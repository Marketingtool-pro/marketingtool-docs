# Shopify Store Audit

AI-powered audit of your Shopify store covering UX, page speed, mobile optimization, trust signals, and conversion bottlenecks.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** Shopify

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/shopify-store-audit](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/shopify-store-audit)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Shopify Store Audit”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Store URL | text | optional |
| Main Issue | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **UX**, **Speed**, **Mobile**.

## Tips for best results

* **Store URL** — Share your Shopify store URL for the AI to audit.
* **Pain Points** — What specific issues are you experiencing?
* **Conversion Rate** — What is your current store conversion rate?

## How to run it

1. Open **Shopify Store Audit** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
