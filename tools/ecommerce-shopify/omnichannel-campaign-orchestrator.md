# Omnichannel Campaign Orchestrator

Orchestrates coordinated marketing campaigns across email, SMS, push, and social ads with unified customer journey mapping.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** E-commerce

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/omnichannel-campaign-orchestrator](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/omnichannel-campaign-orchestrator)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Omnichannel Campaign Orchestrator”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Campaign Name | text | optional |
| Marketing Channels | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Journey**, **Channels**, **Schedule**.

## Tips for best results

* **Active Channels** — List all marketing channels you use.
* **Campaign Goal** — Launch, sale, seasonal, or always-on?
* **Audience Segments** — Who are you targeting — new, returning, VIP?

## How to run it

1. Open **Omnichannel Campaign Orchestrator** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
