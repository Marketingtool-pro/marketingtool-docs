# Creative Fatigue Detector

Monitors ad creative performance decay, alerts when ads are fatiguing, and auto-suggests refresh strategies with new variations.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** E-commerce

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/creative-fatigue-detector](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/creative-fatigue-detector)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Creative Fatigue Detector”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Store / Brand Name | text | optional |
| Ad Platform | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Monitor**, **Alerts**, **Refresh**.

## Tips for best results

* **Active Creatives** — How many ad creatives are currently running?
* **Fatigue Threshold** — When do you consider a creative fatigued — CTR drop, frequency?
* **Creative Types** — Images, videos, carousels, or all?

## How to run it

1. Open **Creative Fatigue Detector** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
