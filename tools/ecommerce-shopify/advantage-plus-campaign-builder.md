# Advantage Plus Campaign Builder

Sets up and optimizes Meta Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns with AI-driven audience targeting and budget allocation.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** E-commerce

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/advantage-plus-campaign-builder](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/advantage-plus-campaign-builder)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Advantage Plus Campaign Builder”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Store / Brand Name | text | optional |
| Daily Budget | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Setup**, **Audience**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Product Catalog** — Is your Meta product catalog connected and up to date?
* **Daily Budget** — What daily budget are you allocating?
* **Target ROAS** — What return on ad spend are you aiming for?

## How to run it

1. Open **Advantage Plus Campaign Builder** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
