# Cart Abandonment Analyzer

Analyzes cart abandonment patterns, identifies top drop-off reasons, and generates targeted recovery strategies beyond basic email.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** E-commerce

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/cart-abandonment-analyzer](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/cart-abandonment-analyzer)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Cart Abandonment Analyzer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Store / Brand Name | text | optional |
| Cart Abandonment Rate | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Patterns**, **Recovery**, **SMS/Push**.

## Tips for best results

* **Current Rate** — Share your current cart abandonment rate.
* **Recovery Tools** — List what recovery methods you currently use.
* **Average Cart Value** — Provide your average cart value for ROI estimation.

## How to run it

1. Open **Cart Abandonment Analyzer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
