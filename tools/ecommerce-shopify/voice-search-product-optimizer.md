# Voice Search Product Optimizer

Optimizes product listings and descriptions for voice search queries, targeting conversational keywords that voice shoppers use.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** E-commerce

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/voice-search-product-optimizer](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/voice-search-product-optimizer)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Voice Search Product Optimizer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Product Name | text | optional |
| Voice Assistant | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Keywords**, **Optimize**, **Preview**.

## Tips for best results

* **Product Type** — What products need voice search optimization?
* **Target Queries** — What questions do shoppers ask about your products?
* **Voice Assistants** — Alexa, Google Assistant, Siri — which matters most?

## How to run it

1. Open **Voice Search Product Optimizer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
