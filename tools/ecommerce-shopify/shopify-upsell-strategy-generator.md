# Shopify Upsell Strategy Generator

Creates upsell and cross-sell strategies for your Shopify store including bundle offers, volume discounts, and post-purchase upsells.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** Shopify

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/shopify-upsell-strategy-generator](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/shopify-upsell-strategy-generator)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Shopify Upsell Strategy Generator”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Store / Brand Name | text | optional |
| Current AOV | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Bundles**, **Upsells**, **Discounts**.

## Tips for best results

* **Best Sellers** — List your top 5 selling products.
* **AOV Goal** — What average order value are you targeting?
* **Current AOV** — What is your current average order value?

## How to run it

1. Open **Shopify Upsell Strategy Generator** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
