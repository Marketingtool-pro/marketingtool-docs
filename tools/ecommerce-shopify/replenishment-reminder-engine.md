# Replenishment Reminder Engine

Predicts product replenishment timing based on purchase frequency and sends automated restock reminders at the optimal moment.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** E-commerce

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/replenishment-reminder-engine](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/replenishment-reminder-engine)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Replenishment Reminder Engine”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Product Name(s) | text | optional |
| Avg Usage Period | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Products**, **Timing**, **Channels**.

## Tips for best results

* **Consumable Products** — Which products need regular replenishment?
* **Avg Usage Period** — How long does a typical supply last?
* **Channels** — Email, SMS, push — which channels do you use?

## How to run it

1. Open **Replenishment Reminder Engine** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
