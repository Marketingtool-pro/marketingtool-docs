# Gross Margin Analyzer

Calculates and tracks gross margin per product and category, flags margin erosion, and recommends pricing adjustments.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** E-commerce

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/gross-margin-analyzer](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/gross-margin-analyzer)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Gross Margin Analyzer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Store / Brand Name | text | optional |
| Target Margin | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Products**, **Erosion**, **Pricing**.

## Tips for best results

* **Product Costs** — Share COGS for your main products.
* **Selling Prices** — Include current retail prices.
* **Margin Target** — What gross margin percentage do you target?

## How to run it

1. Open **Gross Margin Analyzer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
