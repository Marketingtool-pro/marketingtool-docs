# ROAS Analyzer

Monitors Return on Ad Spend across all channels, identifies underperforming campaigns, and suggests budget reallocation for maximum efficiency.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** E-commerce

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/roas-analyzer](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/roas-analyzer)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “ROAS Analyzer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Store / Brand Name | text | optional |
| Monthly Ad Spend | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Channels**, **Budget**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **List Ad Channels** — Include all platforms you advertise on.
* **Share Spend Data** — Provide monthly ad spend per channel.
* **Revenue Attribution** — Note how you track conversions per channel.

## How to run it

1. Open **ROAS Analyzer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
