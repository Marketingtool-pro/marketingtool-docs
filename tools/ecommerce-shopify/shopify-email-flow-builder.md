# Shopify Email Flow Builder

Creates complete Klaviyo/Omnisend email automation flows — abandoned cart, welcome, win-back — tailored to your Shopify store data.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** Shopify

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/shopify-email-flow-builder](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/shopify-email-flow-builder)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Shopify Email Flow Builder”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Store / Brand Name | text | optional |
| Email Platform | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Flows**, **Emails**, **Preview**.

## Tips for best results

* **Email Platform** — Klaviyo, Omnisend, Mailchimp, or Shopify Email?
* **Priority Flow** — Which flow is most important — cart, welcome, or winback?
* **Brand Voice** — Describe your brand tone for email copy.

## How to run it

1. Open **Shopify Email Flow Builder** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
