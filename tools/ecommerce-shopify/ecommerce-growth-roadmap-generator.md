# Ecommerce Growth Roadmap Generator

Generates a customized 12-month growth plan covering marketing channels, budget allocation, and milestone targets for your store.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** E-commerce

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ecommerce-growth-roadmap-generator](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ecommerce-growth-roadmap-generator)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Ecommerce Growth Roadmap Generator”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Store / Brand Name | text | optional |
| Revenue Target | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Roadmap**, **Milestones**, **Budget**.

## Tips for best results

* **Current Revenue** — What is your current monthly revenue?
* **Growth Target** — Where do you want to be in 12 months?
* **Budget** — What is your monthly marketing budget?

## How to run it

1. Open **Ecommerce Growth Roadmap Generator** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
