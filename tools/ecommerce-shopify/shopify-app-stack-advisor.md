# Shopify App Stack Advisor

Recommends the optimal marketing app stack — email, SMS, analytics, reviews — for your Shopify store based on revenue tier and goals.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** Shopify

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/shopify-app-stack-advisor](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/shopify-app-stack-advisor)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Shopify App Stack Advisor”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Store / Brand Name | text | optional |
| Monthly Revenue | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Apps**, **Compare**, **Budget**.

## Tips for best results

* **Monthly Revenue** — What is your store's monthly revenue tier?
* **Current Apps** — List apps you currently use.
* **Pain Points** — What marketing tasks feel hardest or most manual?

## How to run it

1. Open **Shopify App Stack Advisor** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
