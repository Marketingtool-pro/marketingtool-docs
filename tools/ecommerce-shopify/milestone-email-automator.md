# Milestone Email Automator

Creates and schedules personalized milestone emails (birthdays, anniversaries, purchase anniversaries) with dynamic discount offers.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** E-commerce

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/milestone-email-automator](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/milestone-email-automator)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Milestone Email Automator”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Store / Brand Name | text | optional |
| Milestone Types | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Milestones**, **Offers**, **Preview**.

## Tips for best results

* **Milestone Types** — Birthday, signup anniversary, first purchase anniversary?
* **Discount Type** — Percentage off, free shipping, or gift with purchase?
* **Send Timing** — Send on the day, or a few days before?

## How to run it

1. Open **Milestone Email Automator** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
