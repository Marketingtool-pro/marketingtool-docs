# Welcome Series Generator

Creates a multi-step automated welcome email series for new subscribers with personalized content and progressive profiling.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** E-commerce

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/welcome-series-generator](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/welcome-series-generator)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Welcome Series Generator”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Store / Brand Name | text | optional |
| Welcome Offer | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Emails**, **Timing**, **Preview**.

## Tips for best results

* **Brand Voice** — Describe your brand tone — friendly, luxe, casual, etc.
* **Welcome Offer** — Do you offer a first-purchase discount?
* **Series Length** — How many emails — 3, 5, or 7-part series?

## How to run it

1. Open **Welcome Series Generator** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
