# Platform Expansion Planner

Analyzes your current sales data and recommends which new platforms (TikTok, Pinterest, Amazon, Snapchat) to expand to and when.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** E-commerce

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/platform-expansion-planner](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/platform-expansion-planner)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Platform Expansion Planner”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Store / Brand Name | text | optional |
| Current Platforms | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Analysis**, **Platforms**, **Timeline**.

## Tips for best results

* **Current Platforms** — Where do you currently sell and advertise?
* **Audience Demo** — What is your target customer age and interests?
* **Budget for Expansion** — How much can you invest in new channels?

## How to run it

1. Open **Platform Expansion Planner** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
