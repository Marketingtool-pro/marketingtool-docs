# Conversion Rate Optimizer

Analyzes your store's conversion funnel and generates AI recommendations to improve conversion rate and mobile checkout flow.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** E-commerce

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/conversion-rate-optimizer](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/conversion-rate-optimizer)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Conversion Rate Optimizer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Store URL | text | optional |
| Current Conversion Rate | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Funnel**, **Mobile**, **Fixes**.

## Tips for best results

* **Share Your URL** — Provide your store URL so the AI can audit your funnel.
* **Current CVR** — Share your current conversion rate for benchmarking.
* **Top Drop-Off** — Mention where most visitors leave if you know.

## How to run it

1. Open **Conversion Rate Optimizer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
