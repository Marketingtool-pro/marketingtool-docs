# Shopify SEO Optimizer

Generates optimized meta titles, descriptions, alt tags, and structured data for every product and collection page on your Shopify store.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** Shopify

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/shopify-seo-optimizer](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/shopify-seo-optimizer)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Shopify SEO Optimizer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Store URL | text | optional |
| Target Keywords | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Meta Tags**, **Schema**, **Audit**.

## Tips for best results

* **Target Keywords** — What keywords do you want to rank for?
* **Product Count** — How many products need SEO optimization?
* **Current Rankings** — What pages currently rank and for which terms?

## How to run it

1. Open **Shopify SEO Optimizer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
