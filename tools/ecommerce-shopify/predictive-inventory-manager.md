# Predictive Inventory Manager

Forecasts product demand using AI, recommends reorder quantities, and identifies slow-moving inventory before it becomes deadstock.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** E-commerce

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/predictive-inventory-manager](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/predictive-inventory-manager)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Predictive Inventory Manager”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Store / Brand Name | text | optional |
| Number of SKUs | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Forecast**, **Reorder**, **Deadstock**.

## Tips for best results

* **SKU Count** — How many unique SKUs do you manage?
* **Lead Time** — What is your average supplier lead time?
* **Seasonality** — Do you have strong seasonal demand patterns?

## How to run it

1. Open **Predictive Inventory Manager** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
