# Shopify Trust Badge Optimizer

Recommends and generates trust badges, secure payment icons, and social proof elements optimized for your store's niche.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** Shopify

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/shopify-trust-badge-optimizer](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/shopify-trust-badge-optimizer)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Shopify Trust Badge Optimizer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Store URL | text | optional |
| Store Niche | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Badges**, **Social Proof**, **Placement**.

## Tips for best results

* **Store Niche** — What do you sell — fashion, electronics, supplements?
* **Trust Issues** — What objections do customers typically have?
* **Existing Badges** — What trust elements do you currently display?

## How to run it

1. Open **Shopify Trust Badge Optimizer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
