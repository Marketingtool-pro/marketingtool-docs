# Post-Purchase Follow-Up Builder

Generates automated post-purchase email flows including review requests, usage tips, and cross-sell recommendations.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** E-commerce

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/post-purchase-follow-up-builder](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/post-purchase-follow-up-builder)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Post-Purchase Follow-Up Builder”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Store / Brand Name | text | optional |
| Avg Delivery Time | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Flow**, **Reviews**, **Cross-Sell**.

## Tips for best results

* **Product Category** — What type of products do customers buy most?
* **Review Platform** — Where do you collect reviews — Yotpo, Judge.me, etc.?
* **Delivery Time** — Average delivery time to set follow-up timing.

## How to run it

1. Open **Post-Purchase Follow-Up Builder** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
