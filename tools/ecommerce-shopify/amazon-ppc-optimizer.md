# Amazon PPC Optimizer

Optimizes Amazon Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands campaigns with AI bid management and keyword harvesting.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** E-commerce

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/amazon-ppc-optimizer](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/amazon-ppc-optimizer)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Amazon PPC Optimizer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Brand / Product Name | text | optional |
| Target ACoS | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Keywords**, **Bids**, **Reports**.

## Tips for best results

* **Campaign Type** — Sponsored Products, Brands, or Display?
* **ACoS Target** — What is your target Advertising Cost of Sales?
* **Product ASINs** — List your main product ASINs to optimize.

## How to run it

1. Open **Amazon PPC Optimizer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
