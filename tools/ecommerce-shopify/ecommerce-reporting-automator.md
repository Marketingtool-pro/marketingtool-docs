# Ecommerce Reporting Automator

Auto-generates weekly/monthly ecommerce performance reports with AI-written insights, trend analysis, and actionable next steps.

**Category:** [E-commerce & Shopify](../ecommerce-shopify.md) · **Badge:** E-commerce

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ecommerce-reporting-automator](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ecommerce-reporting-automator)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Ecommerce Reporting Automator”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Store / Brand Name | text | optional |
| Report Frequency | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Schedule**, **Template**, **Export**.

## Tips for best results

* **Report Frequency** — Weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly reports?
* **Key Metrics** — Which KPIs matter most to your team?
* **Audience** — Who reads these reports — CEO, marketing team, investors?

## How to run it

1. Open **Ecommerce Reporting Automator** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
