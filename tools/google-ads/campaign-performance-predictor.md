# Campaign Performance Predictor

Predict campaign performance before you launch. Input your targeting, budget, and bid strategy to get estimated impressions, clicks, conversions, CPA, and ROAS projections.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Campaign

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/campaign-performance-predictor](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/campaign-performance-predictor)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Campaign Performance Predictor”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campaign Plan | textarea | ✅ |
| Planned Daily Budget ($) | number | ✅ |
| Industry | select — Ecommerce, SaaS, Legal, Healthcare, Real Estate, Finance | optional |
| Primary Goal | select — Conversions, Revenue, Leads, Brand Awareness | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Setup**, **Monitor**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Be Specific About Audience** — The more detail about targeting, the better the prediction.
* **Include Competition Level** — Competitive niches have higher CPCs and lower conversion rates.
* **Factor in Seasonality** — Mention if you're launching during a peak or off-peak period.

## How to run it

1. Open **Campaign Performance Predictor** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
