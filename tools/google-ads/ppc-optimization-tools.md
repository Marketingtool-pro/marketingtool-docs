# PPC Optimization Suite

Scale your campaigns, not your workload. Complete PPC optimization covering bids, keywords, ads, audiences, and landing pages with impact-ranked recommendations.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** PPC Optimization

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ppc-optimization-tools](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ppc-optimization-tools)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “PPC Optimization Suite”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current Performance | textarea | ✅ |
| Optimization Goal | select — Lower CPA, Higher ROAS, More Conversions, Better Quality Scores, Scale Spend Efficiently | ✅ |
| Monthly Spend ($) | number | optional |
| Current CPA ($) | number | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Quick Wins**, **Advanced**, **Roadmap**.

## Tips for best results

* **Start with Quick Wins** — Focus on high-impact, low-effort changes first.
* **Share Performance Data** — Include CTR, CPA, and ROAS for the last 30 days.
* **Note Your Goals** — Specify if you want more volume or better efficiency.

## How to run it

1. Open **PPC Optimization Suite** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
