# Google Ads Performance Grader

Grade your Google Ads account performance across 10 key areas: Quality Score, click-through rate, impression share, wasted spend, long-tail keywords, and more. Get a letter grade with fixes.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Grader

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/google-ads-performance-grader](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/google-ads-performance-grader)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Google Ads Performance Grader”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account Performance Data | textarea | ✅ |
| Monthly Spend ($) | number | optional |
| Industry | text | optional |
| Grade Focus | select — Full Account Grade, Quality Score Focus, Wasted Spend Focus, CTR Improvement, Competitive Analysis | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Analysis**, **Scoring**, **Fixes**.

## Tips for best results

* **Share Honest Data** — Accurate metrics = accurate grading and recommendations.
* **Include Competitor Context** — Mention your main competitors for benchmark comparison.
* **Grade Monthly** — Track your grade over time to measure improvement.

## How to run it

1. Open **Google Ads Performance Grader** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
