# PPC Budget Calculator

Calculate the optimal PPC budget for your goals. Enter your target CPA, desired conversions, and industry benchmarks to get recommended monthly and daily budgets with confidence ranges.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Budget

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ppc-budget-tool](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ppc-budget-tool)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “PPC Budget Calculator”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Budget Requirements | textarea | ✅ |
| Monthly Conversion Target | number | ✅ |
| Target CPA ($) | number | optional |
| Industry | select — Ecommerce, SaaS/Software, Legal, Healthcare, Real Estate, Education | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Calculate**, **Scenarios**, **Forecast**.

## Tips for best results

* **Start with Goals** — Define how many conversions you need per month.
* **Factor in CPA** — Budget = Target Conversions x Average CPA. Simple but effective.
* **Add 20% Buffer** — Account for CPC fluctuations with a safety margin.

## How to run it

1. Open **PPC Budget Calculator** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
