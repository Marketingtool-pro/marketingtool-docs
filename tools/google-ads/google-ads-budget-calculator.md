# Google Ads Budget Calculator

Calculate the right Google Ads budget for your business goals. Input your target CPA, conversion targets, and industry to get recommended daily and monthly budgets with ROI projections.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Google Ads

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/google-ads-budget-calculator](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/google-ads-budget-calculator)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Google Ads Budget Calculator”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Budget Requirements | textarea | ✅ |
| Monthly Conversion Target | number | ✅ |
| Target CPA ($) | number | optional |
| Industry | select — Ecommerce, SaaS/Software, Legal, Healthcare, Real Estate, Education | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Keywords**, **Bidding**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Start with Goals** — Budget = Target Conversions x Target CPA. Work backwards.
* **Add 20% Buffer** — CPC fluctuates—budget 20% extra for consistent delivery.
* **Test with 3-Month Commitment** — Google Ads needs 2-4 weeks to optimize; plan for 3 months.

## How to run it

1. Open **Google Ads Budget Calculator** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
