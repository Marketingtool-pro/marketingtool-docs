# Performance Booster

Find hidden opportunities faster. Quick-action performance improvements that identify the top changes for the biggest impact on your campaign performance this week.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** PPC Optimization

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ppc-performance-boost](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ppc-performance-boost)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Performance Booster”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What needs boosting? | textarea | ✅ |
| Timeframe | select — This Week, This Month, Next 90 Days | optional |
| Campaign Names | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Actions**, **Impact**, **Track**.

## Tips for best results

* **Focus on Top Campaigns** — Boosting your top 3 campaigns will have the biggest impact.
* **Check Device Performance** — Often a quick device bid adjustment can save 20%+ spend.
* **Review Search Terms Weekly** — Adding negatives from search terms is the #1 quick win.

## How to run it

1. Open **Performance Booster** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
