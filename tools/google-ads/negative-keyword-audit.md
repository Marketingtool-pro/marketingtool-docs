# Negative Keyword Audit

Audit your negative keyword lists for gaps and conflicts. Find search terms wasting budget that need negatives, and check for negatives accidentally blocking profitable keywords.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Audit

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/negative-keyword-audit](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/negative-keyword-audit)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Negative Keyword Audit”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Negative Keywords & Search Terms | textarea | ✅ |
| Audit Focus | select — Find Missing Negatives, Check for Conflicts, Review Match Types, Full Audit | ✅ |
| Monthly Spend ($) | number | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Analysis**, **Scoring**, **Fixes**.

## Tips for best results

* **Check for Conflicts** — Negative keywords can accidentally block your target keywords.
* **Review Weekly** — New irrelevant search terms appear constantly.
* **Use Shared Lists** — Account-level negative lists prevent duplication across campaigns.

## How to run it

1. Open **Negative Keyword Audit** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
