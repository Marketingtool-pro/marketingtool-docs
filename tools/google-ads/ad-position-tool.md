# Ad Position Analyzer

Analyze your ad position data to find the sweet spot between visibility and cost. Understand how position impacts CTR, CPA, and conversion rate for each campaign.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Google Ads

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ad-position-tool](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ad-position-tool)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Ad Position Analyzer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campaign & Position Data | textarea | ✅ |
| Position Goal | select — Top of Page, Absolute Top, Cost-Efficient Position, Maximum Visibility | optional |
| Monthly Budget ($) | number | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Analysis**, **Optimize**, **Compare**.

## Tips for best results

* **Position 1 Isn't Always Best** — Position 2-3 often delivers better CPA than top slot.
* **Check by Campaign** — Optimal position varies by campaign type and keyword intent.
* **Factor in Impression Share** — Low impression share may mean you're being outbid.

## How to run it

1. Open **Ad Position Analyzer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
