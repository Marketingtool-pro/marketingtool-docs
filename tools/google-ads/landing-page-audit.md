# Landing Page Audit

Audit your Google Ads landing pages for conversion rate optimization. Check page speed, mobile experience, message match with ads, form design, and trust signals.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Audit

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/landing-page-audit](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/landing-page-audit)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Landing Page Audit”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Landing Page URL | textarea | ✅ |
| Primary Landing Page URL | text | ✅ |
| Conversion Goal | select — Form Submission, Purchase, Phone Call, App Download, Sign Up | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Analysis**, **Scoring**, **Fixes**.

## Tips for best results

* **Test Page Speed** — Pages loading > 3 seconds lose 53% of mobile visitors.
* **Match Ad to Landing Page** — Headline on page must match the ad that brought them there.
* **Single CTA Focus** — One clear call-to-action converts better than multiple options.

## How to run it

1. Open **Landing Page Audit** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
