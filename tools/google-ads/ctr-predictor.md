# Ctr Predictor

Predict click-through rates for your Google Ads before going live. Input ad copy, keywords, and position data to estimate CTR and identify improvements to boost clicks.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** PPC Optimization

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ctr-predictor](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ctr-predictor)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Ctr Predictor”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ad Copy to Predict | textarea | ✅ |
| Expected Position | select — Absolute Top (1), Top (2-3), Bottom of Page (4-7), Unknown | optional |
| Industry | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Analysis**, **Optimize**, **Report**.

## Tips for best results

* **Test Multiple Headlines** — Enter several headline variations to compare predicted CTR.
* **Include Numbers** — Ads with numbers ('Save 30%', 'Free in 24hrs') get higher CTR.
* **Add Urgency** — Time-sensitive language ('Today Only', 'Limited') boosts clicks.

## How to run it

1. Open **Ctr Predictor** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
