# Marketing Budget Planner

Plan your marketing budget across Google Ads, Meta, and other channels. Get data-driven allocation recommendations based on your goals, industry benchmarks, and historical performance.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Budget

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/marketing-budget-planner](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/marketing-budget-planner)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Marketing Budget Planner”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marketing Plan | textarea | ✅ |
| Total Marketing Budget ($) | number | ✅ |
| Active Channels | text | optional |
| Primary Goal | select — Lead Generation, Ecommerce Sales, Brand Awareness, App Installs, Balanced | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Allocation**, **Forecast**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Allocate 60/40** — Put 60% on proven channels, 40% on testing new ones.
* **Plan Quarterly** — Quarterly planning allows for seasonal and performance adjustments.
* **Track by Channel** — Measure ROI per channel to optimize allocation over time.

## How to run it

1. Open **Marketing Budget Planner** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
