# On Page Seo Checker

Check your landing pages for on-page SEO factors that affect Google Ads Quality Score. Analyze page titles, meta descriptions, content relevance, load speed, and mobile-friendliness.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Audit

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/on-page-seo-checker](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/on-page-seo-checker)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “On Page Seo Checker”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Page URL | textarea | ✅ |
| Primary URL | text | ✅ |
| Target Keywords | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Analysis**, **Scoring**, **Fixes**.

## Tips for best results

* **Match Keywords in H1** — Your main headline should include the target keyword naturally.
* **Check Mobile Score** — Google prioritizes mobile experience for Quality Score.
* **Speed Matters** — Aim for < 2.5 second Largest Contentful Paint (LCP).

## How to run it

1. Open **On Page Seo Checker** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
