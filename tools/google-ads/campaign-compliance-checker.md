# Campaign Compliance Checker

Check your Google Ads campaigns for policy compliance issues before they get disapproved. Scan ad copy, landing pages, and targeting settings against Google's advertising policies.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Audit

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/campaign-compliance-checker](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/campaign-compliance-checker)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Campaign Compliance Checker”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campaign to Check | textarea | ✅ |
| Industry | select — Healthcare, Finance, Legal, Gambling, Alcohol, Education | ✅ |
| Ad Type | select — Search Ads, Display Ads, Shopping Ads, Video Ads | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Analysis**, **Scoring**, **Fixes**.

## Tips for best results

* **Check Before Launching** — Pre-check saves time vs fixing disapprovals after launch.
* **Review Landing Pages** — Google checks landing pages too—ensure they match ad claims.
* **Know Your Industry Rules** — Healthcare, finance, and legal have extra restrictions.

## How to run it

1. Open **Campaign Compliance Checker** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
