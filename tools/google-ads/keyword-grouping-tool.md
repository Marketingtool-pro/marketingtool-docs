# Keyword Grouping Tool

Automatically group keywords into tightly themed ad groups. Build SKAG or STAG structures that maximize Quality Score and ad relevance for better CPC and conversion rates.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Google Ads

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/keyword-grouping-tool](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/keyword-grouping-tool)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Keyword Grouping Tool”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keywords to Group | textarea | ✅ |
| Grouping Strategy | select — SKAG (Single Keyword Ad Groups), STAG (Single Theme Ad Groups), Intent-Based Groups, Auto-Group by Similarity | ✅ |
| Max Keywords Per Group | number | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Group**, **Review**, **Export**.

## Tips for best results

* **3-10 Keywords Per Group** — Keep ad groups tight for maximum ad relevance.
* **Group by Intent** — Separate research, comparison, and purchase-intent keywords.
* **Use N-Gram Analysis** — Group by shared root words for natural ad group themes.

## How to run it

1. Open **Keyword Grouping Tool** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
