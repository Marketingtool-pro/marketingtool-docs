# Ai Campaign Manager

AI-driven campaign management that monitors, adjusts, and optimizes your Google Ads campaigns automatically. Set goals and let AI handle bid adjustments, budget shifts, and ad rotations.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Campaign

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ai-campaign-manager](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ai-campaign-manager)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Ai Campaign Manager”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campaign Goals | textarea | ✅ |
| Campaign Type | select — Search, Shopping, Display, Performance Max, All Types | ✅ |
| Target CPA ($) | number | optional |
| Monthly Budget ($) | number | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Setup**, **Monitor**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Set Clear KPIs** — Define target CPA, ROAS, or conversion volume before automation.
* **Allow Learning Time** — Give AI 2-4 weeks of data before evaluating results.
* **Set Guardrails** — Define max CPA and min ROAS limits to prevent overspend.

## How to run it

1. Open **Ai Campaign Manager** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
