# Shopping Campaign Intelligence

Competitive intelligence for Google Shopping campaigns. Analyze pricing strategies, product feed optimization, and competitor ad positions to win more clicks and conversions in Shopping.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Campaign

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/shopping-campaign-intelligence](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/shopping-campaign-intelligence)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Shopping Campaign Intelligence”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shopping Campaign Details | textarea | ✅ |
| Number of Products | number | optional |
| Intelligence Focus | select — Competitor Pricing, Feed Optimization, Bid Strategy, Product Segmentation, Full Analysis | optional |
| Target ROAS (%) | number | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Setup**, **Monitor**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Segment by Performance** — Group products into heroes, zombies, and villains by ROAS.
* **Optimize Product Titles** — Include brand, product type, and key attributes in titles.
* **Use Supplemental Feeds** — Add custom labels and extra attributes for better targeting.

## How to run it

1. Open **Shopping Campaign Intelligence** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
