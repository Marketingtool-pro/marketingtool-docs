# Budget Manager

Centralized budget management for all your Google Ads campaigns. Track daily pacing, prevent overspend, redistribute budget to top performers, and forecast monthly costs.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Budget

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/budget-manager](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/budget-manager)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Budget Manager”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Budget Overview | textarea | ✅ |
| Monthly Budget ($) | number | ✅ |
| Number of Campaigns | number | optional |
| Main Issue | select — Overspending, Underspending, Uneven Distribution, No Budget Tracking | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Allocation**, **Forecast**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Check Pacing Daily** — Daily checks prevent end-of-month budget crunches.
* **Prioritize by ROI** — Allocate more budget to campaigns with the highest ROAS.
* **Account for Weekends** — B2B campaigns often waste budget on weekends—adjust accordingly.

## How to run it

1. Open **Budget Manager** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
