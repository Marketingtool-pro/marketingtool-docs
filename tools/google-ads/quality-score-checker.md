# Quality Score Checker

Quick check of Quality Score health for any keyword or ad group. Instantly see which QS component (CTR, relevance, landing page) is dragging performance down and get targeted fixes.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Audit

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/quality-score-checker](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/quality-score-checker)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Quality Score Checker”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keywords to Check | textarea | ✅ |
| Current Ad Copy | text | optional |
| Landing Page URL | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Analysis**, **Scoring**, **Fixes**.

## Tips for best results

* **Check All Three Components** — QS is made of CTR, relevance, and landing page—fix the weakest.
* **QS 7+ Is Good** — Above 7 means you're getting CPC discounts. Below 5 means surcharges.
* **Relevance = Keyword in Ad** — The easiest fix: include the keyword in your headline.

## How to run it

1. Open **Quality Score Checker** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
