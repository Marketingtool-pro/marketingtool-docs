# Auto Scaling Budget Tool

Automatically scale budgets based on campaign performance. Increase spend on high-ROAS campaigns and reduce spend on underperformers with configurable rules and safety limits.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Budget

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/auto-scaling-budget-tool](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/auto-scaling-budget-tool)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Auto Scaling Budget Tool”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scaling Strategy | textarea | ✅ |
| Scaling Direction | select — Scale Up Winners, Scale Down Losers, Both, Redistribute Budget | ✅ |
| Max Budget Increase (%) | number | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Allocation**, **Forecast**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Scale Gradually** — Increase budgets by 15-20% at a time to maintain performance.
* **Wait 7 Days Between Changes** — Allow Smart Bidding to adjust to new budget levels.
* **Set Max Limits** — Always cap maximum daily budget to prevent runaway spend.

## How to run it

1. Open **Auto Scaling Budget Tool** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
