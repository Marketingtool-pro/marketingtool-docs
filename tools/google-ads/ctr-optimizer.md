# CTR Optimization Tool

Improve your Google Ads click-through rates with data-driven recommendations. Analyze ad copy, extensions, and positioning to find exactly what's suppressing your CTR.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** PPC Optimization

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ctr-optimizer](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ctr-optimizer)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “CTR Optimization Tool”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CTR Data | textarea | ✅ |
| Current Average CTR (%) | number | optional |
| Ad Format | select — Responsive Search Ads, Expanded Text Ads, Display Ads, Shopping Ads, Video Ads | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Analysis**, **Fixes**, **Predict**.

## Tips for best results

* **Share Current CTR** — Include campaign and ad group level CTR for targeted advice.
* **Include Industry Benchmarks** — Mention your industry so we can compare against averages.
* **Test Headlines First** — Headlines drive 80% of CTR—focus optimization there.

## How to run it

1. Open **CTR Optimization Tool** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
