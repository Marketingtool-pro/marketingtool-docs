# Multi Platform Campaign Hub

Manage campaigns across Google Ads, Microsoft Ads, and other platforms from a single hub. Sync settings, compare performance cross-platform, and manage budgets centrally.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Campaign

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/multi-platform-campaign-hub](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/multi-platform-campaign-hub)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Multi Platform Campaign Hub”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-Platform Setup | textarea | ✅ |
| Active Platforms | text | ✅ |
| Cross-Platform Goal | select — Unified Reporting, Budget Optimization, Campaign Sync, Performance Comparison, All | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Setup**, **Monitor**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Don't Copy Settings Directly** — Each platform has different auction dynamics and audiences.
* **Unified Tracking** — Use UTM parameters consistently across all platforms.
* **Compare Incrementally** — Some platforms drive assisted conversions for others.

## How to run it

1. Open **Multi Platform Campaign Hub** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
