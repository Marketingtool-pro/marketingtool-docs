# Ad Audit Tool

Audit every ad in your Google Ads account. Identify underperforming ads, policy violations, missing extensions, and ad copy issues across all campaigns with actionable fix recommendations.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Audit

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ad-audit-tool](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ad-audit-tool)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Ad Audit Tool”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ads to Audit | textarea | ✅ |
| Audit Focus | select — Ad Copy Quality, Policy Compliance, Extension Coverage, Performance Analysis, Full Audit | optional |
| Industry | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Performance**, **Budget**, **Fixes**.

## Tips for best results

* **Include All Variants** — Audit all active ad variations, not just top performers.
* **Check Extensions** — Missing sitelinks or callouts can hurt Ad Rank by 15-20%.
* **Review Disapprovals** — Fix policy violations immediately to avoid account suspensions.

## How to run it

1. Open **Ad Audit Tool** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
