# Campaign Auto Optimizer

Automatically optimize your campaigns based on performance data. Adjust bids, pause low performers, reallocate budgets, and update ad schedules without manual intervention.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Campaign

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/campaign-auto-optimizer](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/campaign-auto-optimizer)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Campaign Auto Optimizer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Optimization Goals | textarea | ✅ |
| Primary Goal | select — Lower CPA, Higher ROAS, More Conversions, Better CTR, Reduce Wasted Spend | ✅ |
| Optimization Frequency | select — Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Setup**, **Monitor**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Define Success Metrics** — Set clear KPIs before enabling auto-optimization.
* **Set Safety Limits** — Cap maximum bid and budget changes to prevent big swings.
* **Review Weekly** — Auto-optimizer works best with human oversight weekly.

## How to run it

1. Open **Campaign Auto Optimizer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
