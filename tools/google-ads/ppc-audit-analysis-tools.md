# PPC Audit & Analysis

Smarter PPC audits with faster results. In-depth analysis covering account structure, keyword conflicts, negative keyword gaps, ad group themes, and bidding strategy alignment.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Audit

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ppc-audit-analysis-tools](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ppc-audit-analysis-tools)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “PPC Audit & Analysis”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account Overview | textarea | ✅ |
| Audit Scope | select — Full Account, Specific Campaigns, Keywords Only, Ad Copy Only, Landing Pages | ✅ |
| Biggest Concern | text | optional |
| Account Age | select — Less than 3 months, 3-12 months, 1-3 years, 3+ years | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Structure**, **Keywords**, **Actions**.

## Tips for best results

* **Export Campaign Data** — Share campaign and ad group names for structural analysis.
* **Include Search Terms** — Export your search terms report for keyword gap analysis.
* **Note Recent Changes** — Mention any recent strategy or structural changes.

## How to run it

1. Open **PPC Audit & Analysis** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
