# Quality Score Improver

Impression-weighted aggregate Quality Score insights. Systematic approach to improving QS across your account with historical trend analysis and component-level action plans.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Grader

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ppc-quality-score](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ppc-quality-score)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Quality Score Improver”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quality Score Data | textarea | ✅ |
| Average Quality Score | number | optional |
| Priority Component | select — Expected CTR, Ad Relevance, Landing Page Experience, All Components | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **QS History**, **Components**, **Action Plan**.

## Tips for best results

* **Track QS Over Time** — Quality Score changes are gradual—track weekly trends.
* **Fix Lowest Component First** — Improving 'Below Average' components has the highest impact.
* **Tighten Ad Groups** — Smaller, themed ad groups naturally improve ad relevance.

## How to run it

1. Open **Quality Score Improver** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
