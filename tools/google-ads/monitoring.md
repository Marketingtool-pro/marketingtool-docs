# PPC Performance Monitor

Never miss an issue or opportunity again. Real-time monitoring of your Google Ads accounts with alerts for performance drops, budget pacing issues, and anomalies.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Google Ads

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/monitoring](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/monitoring)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “PPC Performance Monitor”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What do you want to monitor? | textarea | ✅ |
| Primary Metric | select — CPA, ROAS, CTR, Conversion Rate, Impression Share, Budget Pacing | ✅ |
| Alert Threshold (%) | number | optional |
| Check Frequency | select — Hourly, Every 6 Hours, Daily, Weekly | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Alerts**, **Trends**, **Settings**.

## Tips for best results

* **Set Thresholds** — Define what % change triggers an alert (e.g. CPA up 30%).
* **Monitor Daily Spend** — Track budget pacing to avoid end-of-month overspend.
* **Watch Conversion Tracking** — Alert on conversion drops—often signals a tracking issue.

## How to run it

1. Open **PPC Performance Monitor** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
