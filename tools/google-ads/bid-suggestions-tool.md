# Bid Suggestions Tool

Get data-driven bid recommendations for every keyword in your account. Based on historical conversion data, competitive landscape, and your target CPA to suggest optimal max CPC bids.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Budget

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/bid-suggestions-tool](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/bid-suggestions-tool)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Bid Suggestions Tool”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keywords & Current Bids | textarea | ✅ |
| Bidding Goal | select — Lower CPA, Higher Position, More Impressions, Better ROAS | ✅ |
| Daily Budget ($) | number | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Analysis**, **Optimize**, **Report**.

## Tips for best results

* **Include Conversion Data** — Share conversion rates per keyword for accurate suggestions.
* **Factor in Margins** — Tell us your profit margins so bids stay profitable.
* **Test Suggestions Gradually** — Apply bid changes to 20% of keywords first, then expand.

## How to run it

1. Open **Bid Suggestions Tool** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
