# Geo Targeting Optimizer

Optimize geographic targeting and bid adjustments in Google Ads. Analyze performance by city, state, and region to increase bids in high-converting areas and cut spend in low-performers.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** PPC Optimization

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/geo-targeting-optimizer](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/geo-targeting-optimizer)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Geo Targeting Optimizer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Geographic Strategy | textarea | ✅ |
| Geographic Scope | select — City-Level, State/Region, Country-Level, Radius Targeting, Global | ✅ |
| Target Locations | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Targeting**, **Performance**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Check Location Settings** — Use 'Presence' not 'Presence or Interest' to avoid irrelevant clicks.
* **Analyze by City** — Performance varies dramatically even within the same state.
* **Exclude Low-Performing Areas** — Excluding bad geos is as important as boosting good ones.

## How to run it

1. Open **Geo Targeting Optimizer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
