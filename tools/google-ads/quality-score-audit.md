# Quality Score Audit

Comprehensive audit of Quality Scores across your Google Ads account. Analyze expected CTR, ad relevance, and landing page experience for every keyword with a prioritized improvement plan.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Audit

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/quality-score-audit](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/quality-score-audit)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Quality Score Audit”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quality Score Data | textarea | ✅ |
| Account Average QS | number | optional |
| Improve First | select — Expected CTR, Ad Relevance, Landing Page Experience, Lowest QS Keywords, Highest Spend Keywords | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Analysis**, **Scoring**, **Fixes**.

## Tips for best results

* **Focus on High-Spend Keywords** — Improving QS on your biggest spenders saves the most money.
* **Fix Below Average First** — Moving from 'Below Average' to 'Average' has the biggest impact.
* **Track QS Weekly** — Quality Score changes are gradual—monitor trends over time.

## How to run it

1. Open **Quality Score Audit** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
