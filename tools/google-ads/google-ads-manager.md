# Google Ads Campaign Manager

All-in-one Google Ads campaign management suite. Monitor performance, manage bids, optimize ad copy, and control budgets across Search, Display, Shopping, and Performance Max campaigns.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Campaign

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/google-ads-manager](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/google-ads-manager)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Google Ads Campaign Manager”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campaign Overview | textarea | ✅ |
| Campaign Type | select — Search, Display, Shopping, Performance Max, Video, App | ✅ |
| Primary Goal | select — Increase Conversions, Lower CPA, Improve ROAS, Scale Spend, Launch New Campaign | optional |
| Monthly Spend ($) | number | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Campaigns**, **Optimize**, **Report**.

## Tips for best results

* **List All Campaigns** — Include campaign names, types, and current performance.
* **Set Clear KPIs** — Define target CPA, ROAS, or conversion goals per campaign.
* **Note Constraints** — Mention any budget limits or bidding restrictions.

## How to run it

1. Open **Google Ads Campaign Manager** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
