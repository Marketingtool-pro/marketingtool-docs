# Campaign Budget Allocator

Intelligently allocate budget across campaigns based on performance, goals, and priority. Automatically shift spend from underperformers to your highest-ROI campaigns.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Campaign

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/campaign-budget-allocator](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/campaign-budget-allocator)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Campaign Budget Allocator”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campaign List | textarea | ✅ |
| Total Monthly Budget ($) | number | ✅ |
| Allocation Strategy | select — Performance-Based, Equal Distribution, Priority-Weighted, ROI-Optimized | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Setup**, **Monitor**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Rank by Priority** — Mark campaigns as high/medium/low priority for smarter allocation.
* **Factor in Conversion Lag** — Some campaigns convert slower—don't cut budget too early.
* **Reserve 10% for Testing** — Keep a portion of budget for testing new campaigns.

## How to run it

1. Open **Campaign Budget Allocator** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
