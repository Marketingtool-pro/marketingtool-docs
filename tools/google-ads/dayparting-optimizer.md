# Dayparting Optimizer

Optimize your ad schedule by hour and day of week. Analyze conversion patterns to increase bids during peak hours, reduce bids during dead zones, and maximize ROI around the clock.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** PPC Optimization

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/dayparting-optimizer](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/dayparting-optimizer)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Dayparting Optimizer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Schedule Analysis Request | textarea | ✅ |
| Timezone | text | ✅ |
| Business Type | select — B2B (Weekday Focus), B2C (All Week), Ecommerce (24/7), Local (Business Hours), Custom | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Targeting**, **Performance**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Analyze 90+ Days** — Need at least 90 days of data for reliable hourly patterns.
* **Check Weekends Separately** — Weekend performance often differs dramatically from weekdays.
* **Don't Over-Restrict** — Cutting too many hours limits Smart Bidding's learning.

## How to run it

1. Open **Dayparting Optimizer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
