# Campaign Audit Tool

Full campaign-level audit of your Google Ads account. Check campaign settings, targeting, bidding strategy alignment, budget allocation, and conversion tracking setup for errors.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Audit

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/campaign-audit-tool](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/campaign-audit-tool)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Campaign Audit Tool”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campaign Details | textarea | ✅ |
| Campaign Type | select — Search, Display, Shopping, Performance Max, Video, All Types | optional |
| Audit Priority | select — Settings & Targeting, Bidding Strategy, Budget Allocation, Conversion Tracking, Full Audit | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Analysis**, **Scoring**, **Fixes**.

## Tips for best results

* **Check Location Targeting** — Ensure 'Presence' not 'Interest' targeting to avoid wasted spend.
* **Verify Conversion Tracking** — Broken tracking = broken bidding. Check tags are firing.
* **Review Network Settings** — Search Partners and Display expansion often waste budget.

## How to run it

1. Open **Campaign Audit Tool** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
