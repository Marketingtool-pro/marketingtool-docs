# Budget Management Tool

Take control of your PPC budgets with real-time spend tracking, projections, alerts, and campaign automation. Ensure your best campaigns always get enough budget while staying within monthly limits.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Budget

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/budget-management](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/budget-management)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Budget Management Tool”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Budget Overview | textarea | ✅ |
| Total Monthly Budget ($) | number | ✅ |
| Number of Active Campaigns | number | optional |
| Primary Goal | select — Maximize Conversions, Minimize CPA, Maximize ROAS, Even Distribution, Brand + Performance Split | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Allocation**, **Forecast**, **Alerts**.

## Tips for best results

* **Set Monthly Cap** — Define your total monthly budget to prevent overspend.
* **Rank Campaigns** — Prioritize campaigns by ROI so budget flows to winners first.
* **Plan for Seasonality** — Mention peak periods so forecasts account for demand spikes.

## How to run it

1. Open **Budget Management Tool** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
