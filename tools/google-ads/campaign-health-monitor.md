# Campaign Health Monitor

Real-time health monitoring for your Google Ads campaigns. Track conversion rates, budget pacing, impression share, and quality scores with alerts when metrics drop below thresholds.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Campaign

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/campaign-health-monitor](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/campaign-health-monitor)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Campaign Health Monitor”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monitoring Setup | textarea | ✅ |
| Alert When | select — CPA Increases 20%+, CTR Drops 15%+, Conversions Drop 25%+, Budget Overspend, Custom Threshold | ✅ |
| Check Frequency | select — Hourly, Every 6 Hours, Daily | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Setup**, **Monitor**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Monitor Conversion Tracking** — A sudden drop in conversions often means broken tracking.
* **Set Budget Pacing Alerts** — Know by mid-month if you'll overspend or underspend.
* **Watch Quality Scores** — QS drops signal ad relevance or landing page issues.

## How to run it

1. Open **Campaign Health Monitor** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
