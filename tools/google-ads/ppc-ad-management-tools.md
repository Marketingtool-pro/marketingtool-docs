# Ad Management Suite

Boost efficiency with smart tools and AI. RSA asset tracking, generative AI for ad copy, and systematic A/B testing to find the highest-converting ad variations.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Google Ads

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ppc-ad-management-tools](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ppc-ad-management-tools)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Ad Management Suite”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ad Brief | textarea | ✅ |
| Ad Type | select — Responsive Search Ads, Display Ads, Video Ads, Shopping Ads, All Types | ✅ |
| Brand Tone | select — Professional, Casual, Urgent, Friendly, Technical, Luxury | optional |
| Call to Action | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Create**, **Test**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Include Brand Voice** — Describe your brand tone: professional, casual, urgent, etc.
* **Add Competitors** — List competitor names so we can differentiate your messaging.
* **Mention USPs** — Share unique selling points to highlight in ad copy.

## How to run it

1. Open **Ad Management Suite** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
