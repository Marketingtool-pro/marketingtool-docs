# PPC Account Auditor

Smarter PPC audits, faster results. Comprehensive audit of your Google Ads account structure, keyword conflicts, negative keyword gaps, and bidding alignment with a prioritized action plan.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Audit

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/auditing](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/auditing)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “PPC Account Auditor”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account Details | textarea | ✅ |
| Google Ads Account ID | text | optional |
| Monthly Ad Spend | select — Under $1,000, $1,000-$5,000, $5,000-$25,000, $25,000-$100,000, $100,000+ | optional |
| Audit Focus | select — Full Account, Campaign Structure, Keywords, Ad Copy, Bidding, Landing Pages | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Issues**, **Opportunities**, **Report**.

## Tips for best results

* **Include Account ID** — Provide your Google Ads account ID for a more specific audit.
* **Set Time Range** — Audit at least 90 days of data for meaningful insights.
* **List Priorities** — Mention if you care more about leads, sales, or brand awareness.

## How to run it

1. Open **PPC Account Auditor** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
