# Placement Optimization

Optimize your Display and YouTube ad placements for maximum performance. Identify top-converting sites, exclude poor performers, and build custom placement lists for targeted reach.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** PPC Optimization

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/placement-optimization](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/placement-optimization)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Placement Optimization”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Placement Strategy | textarea | ✅ |
| Optimization Goal | select — Find Best Placements, Exclude Bad Placements, Build Custom Lists, Improve Conversion Rate, Full Optimization | ✅ |
| Display/Video Budget ($) | number | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Targeting**, **Performance**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Use Managed Placements** — Hand-picked placements outperform automatic targeting.
* **Exclude Mobile Games** — Gaming apps generate accidental clicks—exclude by default.
* **Test Placement Categories** — Group placements by topic to find which categories convert best.

## How to run it

1. Open **Placement Optimization** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
