# Shopify Campaign Manager

AI-powered shopify campaign manager.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Campaign

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/shopify-campaign-manager](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/shopify-campaign-manager)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Shopify Campaign Manager”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Setup**, **Monitor**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Define Objective** — Specify campaign goal: awareness, traffic, or conversions.
* **Set Budget Range** — Include your budget for realistic recommendations.
* **Name Your Audience** — Describe your ideal customer profile.

## How to run it

1. Open **Shopify Campaign Manager** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
