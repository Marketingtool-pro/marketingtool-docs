# Performance Max Optimizer

Optimize your Performance Max campaigns across all Google channels. Analyze asset group performance, audience signals, and search categories to maximize conversions.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Campaign

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/performance-max](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/performance-max)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Performance Max Optimizer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PMax Campaign Details | textarea | ✅ |
| Campaign Objective | select — Online Sales, Lead Generation, Store Visits, Brand Awareness | ✅ |
| Number of Asset Groups | number | optional |
| Daily Budget ($) | number | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Assets**, **Audiences**, **Insights**.

## Tips for best results

* **Share Asset Groups** — List your asset groups and their performance metrics.
* **Include Audience Signals** — Mention your custom segments and audience signals.
* **Note Conversion Actions** — Specify which conversions you're optimizing for.

## How to run it

1. Open **Performance Max Optimizer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
