# Bid Optimization Engine

Optimize bids across your Google Ads account with advanced algorithms. Adjust bids by keyword, device, location, time of day, and audience to maximize conversions within your budget.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Budget

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/bid-optimization-engine](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/bid-optimization-engine)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Bid Optimization Engine”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bid Strategy Details | textarea | ✅ |
| Current Strategy | select — Manual CPC, Enhanced CPC, Target CPA, Target ROAS, Maximize Conversions, Maximize Clicks | ✅ |
| Target CPA ($) | number | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Analysis**, **Optimize**, **Report**.

## Tips for best results

* **Segment by Device** — Mobile and desktop often need very different bid adjustments.
* **Use Dayparting** — Reduce bids during low-conversion hours to save budget.
* **Monitor Auction Insights** — Know when competitors increase bids so you can respond.

## How to run it

1. Open **Bid Optimization Engine** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
