# Campaign Template Library

Pre-built campaign templates for every Google Ads campaign type. Launch new Search, Shopping, Display, and Performance Max campaigns with proven structures, settings, and best practices.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Campaign

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/campaign-template-library](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/campaign-template-library)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Campaign Template Library”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What are you advertising? | textarea | ✅ |
| Campaign Type | select — Search - Lead Gen, Search - Ecommerce, Shopping - Standard, Shopping - PMax, Display - Remarketing, Display - Prospecting | ✅ |
| Daily Budget ($) | number | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Setup**, **Monitor**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Start with Proven Templates** — Templates encode best practices—customize from there.
* **Adapt to Your Industry** — Modify bid strategies and targeting for your specific niche.
* **Include Negative Keywords** — Templates include starter negative keyword lists.

## How to run it

1. Open **Campaign Template Library** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
