# Campaign Scaling Assistant

Safely scale your winning Google Ads campaigns without killing performance. Get a step-by-step scaling plan with budget increases, bid adjustments, and new keyword expansion strategies.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Campaign

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/campaign-scaling-assistant](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/campaign-scaling-assistant)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Campaign Scaling Assistant”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campaign to Scale | textarea | ✅ |
| Current Monthly Spend ($) | number | ✅ |
| Target Monthly Spend ($) | number | optional |
| Key Constraint | select — Keep CPA Under Target, Maintain ROAS, Maximize Volume, Stay Within Budget | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Setup**, **Monitor**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Scale 15-20% Per Week** — Gradual increases maintain performance; big jumps crash it.
* **Expand Keywords First** — Add new keywords before increasing bids on existing ones.
* **Test New Match Types** — Broad match with Smart Bidding can unlock new volume.

## How to run it

1. Open **Campaign Scaling Assistant** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
