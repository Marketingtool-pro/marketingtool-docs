# Quality Score Analyzer

The ultimate guide to Google Ads Quality Score. Deep-dive into expected CTR, ad relevance, and landing page experience with impression-weighted aggregate insights and specific fixes.

**Category:** [Google Ads & PPC](../google-ads.md) · **Badge:** Grader

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/google-ads-quality-score](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/google-ads-quality-score)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Quality Score Analyzer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keywords & Quality Scores | textarea | ✅ |
| Landing Page URL | text | optional |
| Current Ad Headlines | text | optional |
| Industry | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Expected CTR**, **Ad Relevance**, **Landing Page**.

## Tips for best results

* **Include Keywords** — List your top keywords with their current Quality Scores.
* **Share Ad Copy** — Paste actual ad text so we can check keyword-to-ad relevance.
* **Add Landing URL** — Include your landing page URL for page experience analysis.

## How to run it

1. Open **Quality Score Analyzer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
