# Roi Calculator

AI-powered roi calculator.

**Category:** [Analytics, ROI & Attribution](../analytics-roi.md) · **Badge:** ROI & Attribution

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/roi-calculator](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/roi-calculator)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Roi Calculator”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Allocation**, **Forecast**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Specify Time Range** — Define the date range for your analysis period.
* **List Key Metrics** — Name the KPIs you care about most.
* **Include Benchmarks** — Add industry benchmarks for comparison context.

## How to run it

1. Open **Roi Calculator** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
