* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/brand-safety-monitor](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/brand-safety-monitor)

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Metrics**, **Insights**, **Export**.

## Tips for best results

* **Specify Time Range** — Define the date range for your analysis period.
* **List Key Metrics** — Name the KPIs you care about most.
* **Include Benchmarks** — Add industry benchmarks for comparison context.

## How to run it

1. Open **Brand Safety Monitor** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
