# Automated Reporting Suite

Automate your PPC reporting with scheduled delivery. Choose templates, customize metrics, add annotations, and auto-send weekly or monthly reports to stakeholders.

**Category:** [Analytics, ROI & Attribution](../analytics-roi.md) · **Badge:** Analytics

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ppc-reporting-tools](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ppc-reporting-tools)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Automated Reporting Suite”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reporting Setup | textarea | ✅ |
| Report Frequency | select — Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly | ✅ |
| Email Recipients | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Templates**, **Schedule**, **Deliver**.

## Tips for best results

* **Use Templates** — Start with a proven template and customize from there.
* **Schedule Consistently** — Weekly reports build trust and keep stakeholders informed.
* **Include Action Items** — End each report with 3-5 recommended next steps.

## How to run it

1. Open **Automated Reporting Suite** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
