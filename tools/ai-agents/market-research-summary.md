# Market Research Summary

Generate market research summaries.

**Category:** [AI Agents & Automation](../ai-agents.md) · **Badge:** Marketing

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/market-research-summary](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/market-research-summary)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Market Research Summary”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Competitors**, **Trends**, **Strategy**.

## Tips for best results

* **Be Detailed** — Provide specific information for accurate Market Research Summary results.
* **Set Clear Goals** — Define what success looks like for your task.
* **Iterate Results** — Refine your input based on initial output.

## How to run it

1. Open **Market Research Summary** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
