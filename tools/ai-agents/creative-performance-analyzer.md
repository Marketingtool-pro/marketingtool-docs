# Creative Performance Analyzer

Deep-dive creative performance analyzer that breaks down your Facebook and Instagram ad performance by visual elements, copy length, CTA type, format, and placement for actionable creative insights.

**Category:** [AI Agents & Automation](../ai-agents.md) · **Badge:** Creative

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/creative-performance-analyzer](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/creative-performance-analyzer)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Creative Performance Analyzer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ad Account or Campaign ID | text | ✅ |
| Breakdown By | select — Creative Format, Copy Length, CTA Type, Visual Elements, Placement, All Dimensions | optional |
| Primary Metric | select — ROAS, CPA, CTR, Engagement Rate, Conversion Rate | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Metrics**, **Insights**, **Export**.

## Tips for best results

* **Compare formats** — See how your static images perform vs. videos vs. carousels across different audiences and placements.
* **Analyze copy length** — Discover whether short punchy copy or longer storytelling drives better results for your products.
* **Check placement fit** — Feed creatives and Stories creatives perform differently — analyze each separately for true insights.

## How to run it

1. Open **Creative Performance Analyzer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
