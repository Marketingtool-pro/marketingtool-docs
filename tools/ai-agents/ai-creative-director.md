# Ai Creative Director

AI-powered creative director that analyzes your brand guidelines, audience preferences, and campaign objectives to generate strategic creative briefs and visual direction for Facebook and Instagram ads.

**Category:** [AI Agents & Automation](../ai-agents.md) · **Badge:** Creative

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ai-creative-director](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ai-creative-director)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Ai Creative Director”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand & Campaign Brief | textarea | ✅ |
| Ad Platform | select — Facebook Feed, Instagram Stories, Instagram Reels, Facebook + Instagram, All Meta Placements | optional |
| Creative Style | select — Lifestyle Photography, Product-Focused, User-Generated Content, Bold Typography, Minimal & Clean, Video-First | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Create**, **Library**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Define brand guidelines** — Upload your brand colors, fonts, and tone of voice so AI can maintain consistency across all creatives.
* **Set campaign themes** — Provide 2-3 campaign themes and let AI generate creative concepts for each direction.
* **Review and iterate** — Use AI suggestions as starting points, then refine based on your brand knowledge and audience insights.

## How to run it

1. Open **Ai Creative Director** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
