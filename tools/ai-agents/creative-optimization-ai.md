# Creative Optimization Ai

AI engine that continuously tests and optimizes your Facebook ad creatives in real-time. Automatically identifies winning visual elements, copy combinations, and formats to maximize conversion rates.

**Category:** [AI Agents & Automation](../ai-agents.md) · **Badge:** Creative

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/creative-optimization-ai](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/creative-optimization-ai)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Creative Optimization Ai”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campaign or Ad Set to Optimize | textarea | ✅ |
| Optimize For | select — Highest CTR, Lowest CPA, Best ROAS, Maximum Engagement, Most Conversions | optional |
| Testing Speed | select — Conservative (7+ days), Standard (3-5 days), Aggressive (1-2 days) | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Create**, **Library**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Test multiple variants** — Upload 5-10 creative variations and let AI identify the top 2-3 performers based on real conversion data.
* **Optimize by audience** — Different audiences respond to different creatives — let AI match the best creative to each segment.
* **Use dynamic creative** — Combine AI optimization with Meta's Dynamic Creative feature for maximum testing velocity.

## How to run it

1. Open **Creative Optimization Ai** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
