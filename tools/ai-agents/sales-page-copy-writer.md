# Sales Page Copy Writer

Write high-converting sales page copy.

**Category:** [AI Agents & Automation](../ai-agents.md) · **Badge:** Marketing

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/sales-page-copy-writer](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/sales-page-copy-writer)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Sales Page Copy Writer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Writing**, **Editing**, **Publish**.

## Tips for best results

* **Define Your Topic** — Be specific about the subject for focused content.
* **Set Target Length** — Specify word count for appropriate depth.
* **Know Your Reader** — Describe your audience for the right tone and level.

## How to run it

1. Open **Sales Page Copy Writer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
