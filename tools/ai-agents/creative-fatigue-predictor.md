# Creative Fatigue Predictor

Predict when your Facebook and Instagram ad creatives will hit fatigue before performance drops. Monitor frequency, engagement decay, and CTR trends to know exactly when to refresh your ads.

**Category:** [AI Agents & Automation](../ai-agents.md) · **Badge:** Creative

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/creative-fatigue-predictor](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/creative-fatigue-predictor)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Creative Fatigue Predictor”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ad Account or Campaign ID | text | ✅ |
| Fatigue Sensitivity | select — Early Warning (Frequency 2+), Standard (Frequency 3+), Late Warning (Frequency 5+) | optional |
| Analysis Period | select — Last 7 days, Last 14 days, Last 30 days, Last 60 days | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Create**, **Library**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Watch frequency closely** — Ads typically fatigue when frequency exceeds 3-4x — this tool alerts you before that threshold.
* **Prepare replacements early** — Start designing replacement creatives when fatigue signals appear, not after performance crashes.
* **Rotate strategically** — Don't replace all creatives at once — stagger refreshes to maintain consistent performance.

## How to run it

1. Open **Creative Fatigue Predictor** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
