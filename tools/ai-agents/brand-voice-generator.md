# Brand Voice Generator

Create consistent brand voice guidelines.

**Category:** [AI Agents & Automation](../ai-agents.md) · **Badge:** Branding

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/brand-voice-generator](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/brand-voice-generator)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Brand Voice Generator”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Product/Brand Name | text | optional |
| Target Market | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Writing**, **Editing**, **Publish**.

## Tips for best results

* **Share Brand Examples** — Include links to existing brand content.
* **List Brand Values** — Name your core values and personality traits.
* **Define Target Market** — Describe your ideal customer.

## How to run it

1. Open **Brand Voice Generator** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
