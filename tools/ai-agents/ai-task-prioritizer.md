# Ai Task Prioritizer

AI-powered ai task prioritizer.

**Category:** [AI Agents & Automation](../ai-agents.md) · **Badge:** AI Agent

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ai-task-prioritizer](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/ai-task-prioritizer)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Ai Task Prioritizer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Configure**, **Execute**, **Results**.

## Tips for best results

* **Define the Task** — Be specific about what the AI should accomplish.
* **Set Parameters** — Include constraints, limits, and success criteria.
* **Provide Context** — Share background data for more accurate results.

## How to run it

1. Open **Ai Task Prioritizer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
