# Creative Intelligence Platform

Comprehensive creative intelligence platform that uses computer vision and machine learning to automatically tag, categorize, and score your ad creatives across Facebook and Instagram campaigns.

**Category:** [AI Agents & Automation](../ai-agents.md) · **Badge:** Creative

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/creative-intelligence-platform](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/creative-intelligence-platform)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Creative Intelligence Platform”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ad Account ID | text | ✅ |
| Analysis Type | select — Full Creative Audit, Top Performers Only, Visual Element Analysis, Format Comparison | optional |
| Creative Format | select — All Formats, Static Images, Videos, Carousels, Stories/Reels | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Metrics**, **Insights**, **Export**.

## Tips for best results

* **Let AI tag visuals** — AI automatically identifies elements in your creatives — people, colors, text overlays, product shots — and correlates with performance.
* **Find winning patterns** — Discover which visual elements (bright colors, faces, lifestyle shots) drive the highest engagement in your niche.
* **Build a creative library** — Organize all your ad assets by performance tier and visual attributes for faster future campaign builds.

## How to run it

1. Open **Creative Intelligence Platform** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
