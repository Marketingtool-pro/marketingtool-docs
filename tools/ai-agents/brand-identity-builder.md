# Brand Identity Builder

AI-powered brand identity builder with logo, colors, and style guide.

**Category:** [AI Agents & Automation](../ai-agents.md) · **Badge:** Branding

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/brand-identity-builder](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/brand-identity-builder)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Brand Identity Builder”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |
| Product/Brand Name | text | optional |
| Target Market | text | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Colors & Type**, **Assets**, **Export**.

## How to run it

1. Open **Brand Identity Builder** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
