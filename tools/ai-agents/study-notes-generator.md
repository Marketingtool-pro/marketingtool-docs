# Study Notes Generator

AI-powered study notes generator.

**Category:** [AI Agents & Automation](../ai-agents.md) · **Badge:** Education

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/study-notes-generator](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/study-notes-generator)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Study Notes Generator”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Describe what you need | textarea | ✅ |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Writing**, **Editing**, **Publish**.

## Tips for best results

* **Name the Subject** — Specify the exact topic or course.
* **Set Difficulty** — Indicate beginner, intermediate, or advanced.
* **Define Format** — Choose notes, flashcards, quiz, or summary.

## How to run it

1. Open **Study Notes Generator** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
