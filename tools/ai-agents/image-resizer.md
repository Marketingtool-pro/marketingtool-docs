# Image Resizer

Intelligent image resizer for Meta ad creatives that automatically adapts your designs to all Facebook and Instagram placement dimensions — Feed (1:1), Stories (9:16), Reels (9:16), and Marketplace — while preserving visual quality.

**Category:** [AI Agents & Automation](../ai-agents.md) · **Badge:** Creative

## Where to find it

* **Web app** — [app.marketingtool.pro/tools/image-resizer](https://app.marketingtool.pro/tools/image-resizer)
* **iOS / Android app** — open the **Tools** tab and search “Image Resizer”

## Inputs

| Input | Type | Required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Image URL or Description | textarea | ✅ |
| Target Placements | select — All Meta Placements, Feed Only (1:1 & 4:5), Stories & Reels (9:16), Marketplace (1:1), Custom Sizes | optional |
| Output Format | select — PNG (Best Quality), JPEG (Smaller Size), WebP (Modern Browsers) | optional |

## Result workspace

Your result page is organized into tabs: **Overview**, **Create**, **Library**, **Optimize**.

## Tips for best results

* **Start with highest res** — Upload the highest resolution version of your creative — resizing down maintains quality, sizing up doesn't.
* **Check focal points** — Ensure your product or key message stays visible after cropping for different aspect ratios.
* **Batch process** — Upload all your creatives at once and generate all placement sizes in a single batch to save time.

## How to run it

1. Open **Image Resizer** (links above).
2. Fill in the inputs and press **Generate**.
3. Review your full result page — it's saved to your History automatically. On desktop, use **Download All** to save every asset.
