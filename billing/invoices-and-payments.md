# Invoices & payments

* **Web subscriptions** — invoices are available from your web dashboard's billing section.
* **iOS purchases** — receipts come from Apple; see your purchase history in the App Store.
* **Android purchases** — receipts come from Google Play; see your order history at pay.google.com.

Need a specific document for accounting? Email [Help@marketingtool.pro](mailto:Help@marketingtool.pro).
