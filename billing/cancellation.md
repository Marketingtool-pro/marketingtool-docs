# Cancellation

* **Web** — cancel from the billing section of your dashboard.
* **iOS** — cancel in **Settings → your name → Subscriptions** (Apple manages the billing).
* **Android** — cancel in **Play Store → Payments & subscriptions**.

After cancellation you keep your plan until the end of the paid period, then move to the free tier. Your account, history, and results are **not** deleted.
