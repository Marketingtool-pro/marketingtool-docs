# Subscriptions

## On the web

Manage your plan from the web dashboard — upgrades and downgrades apply immediately.

## On iOS

Subscriptions are **in-app purchases handled by Apple**. Manage or cancel them in **Settings → your name → Subscriptions** on your device. Apple bills you directly.

## On Android

Subscriptions are handled by **Google Play**. Manage or cancel them in **Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions**.

## One plan everywhere

Whichever platform you subscribe on, your plan applies to your whole account — web and both apps.
