# Apps & platforms

## Web app

[app.marketingtool.pro/dashboard](https://app.marketingtool.pro/dashboard) is the full-size experience:

* Rich HTML result pages designed for a 1920px desktop screen
* **Download All** for every generated asset
* Account, billing, and history management

## Mobile apps

The iOS and Android apps are **identical in features and interface** — same tools, same navigation, same results.

* **Get Started**, **Help**, and **Tutorial** links in the apps open the corresponding web-app pages, so you always land in the right place.
* Started something on your phone? Open the web app on your desktop and pick it up at full size.

## Continuity

Your account, history, and credits are shared across all platforms in real time.
