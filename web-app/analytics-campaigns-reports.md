# Analytics, campaigns & reports

## Analytics

Track the KPIs that matter across your connected channels. Use it together with the [Analytics & ROI tools](../tools/analytics-roi.md) for deeper AI-driven analysis.

## Campaigns

Manage campaigns across platforms from one place — create, review, and optimize without hopping between ad managers.

## Reports

Every report you generate is stored here, ready to review or share with clients.

## Ad Library

All the ad creatives you've generated, browsable in one place — reuse and iterate instead of starting from scratch.

## Meta Audit

A dedicated audit workspace for Meta ad accounts: run the audit, review the findings, act on them with the matching [Facebook & Meta tools](../tools/facebook-meta.md).

## Command Centre

The power view: everything running in your account — jobs, automations, and recent results — on one screen.
