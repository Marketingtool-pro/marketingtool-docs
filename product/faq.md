# FAQ

## Are all tools available on the free plan?

Yes. Every tool is open on every plan — plans only set your usage quota. See [Plans & credits](../getting-started/plans-and-credits.md).

## Is the credit balance I see real?

Yes. The balance shown in the app is your live, actual balance.

## Do the mobile apps have fewer features than the web?

No — both apps mirror the web tool catalog with the same interface. The web app adds desktop-sized result pages and **Download All**.

## Which AI does MarketingTool.Pro use?

MarketingTool.Pro runs its own AI pipeline tuned for marketing work, combining multiple state-of-the-art models behind the scenes with automatic failover, so results stay fast and reliable.

## Can I use my results commercially?

Yes — everything you generate with your account is yours to use.

## How do I get support?

Ask MarketBot in the app, or email [Help@marketingtool.pro](mailto:Help@marketingtool.pro).
