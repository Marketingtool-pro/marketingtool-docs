# Troubleshooting

## I didn't receive my sign-in code

Check your spam folder, then request a new code — codes expire after a few minutes. Corporate mail filters can delay delivery; a personal address is a quick workaround.

## A tool result failed or looks incomplete

Re-run the tool — transient failures are automatically retried on our side, but a fresh run is the fastest fix. Failed runs are not meant to consume your credits; if you believe you were charged for a failure, contact support with the tool name and time.

## The app can't reach the server

Check your connection, then force-close and reopen the app. If the problem persists, check the web app at [app.marketingtool.pro](https://app.marketingtool.pro) — if the web works, reinstalling the app usually resolves it.

## My subscription isn't recognized

Make sure you're signed in with the same account you subscribed with. iOS: **Settings → Subscriptions**; Android: **Play Store → Payments & subscriptions** to confirm the subscription is active, then reopen the app.

## Something else?

Email [Help@marketingtool.pro](mailto:Help@marketingtool.pro) with your account email, platform (web / iOS / Android), and a screenshot if you can.
